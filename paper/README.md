# Journal manuscript plan (no paper yet)

Only begin a complete paper after a real result has been proved and passed initial novelty, with mathlib evidence clearly marked where not yet ready. Target a well-motivated HHA-level theorem, substantive new topological/homological methods, examples and limitations, full proof, comparison with closest literature, references, and concise exposition.

Publisher-indexed [author guidance](https://intlpress.com/site/pub/editorauthorpages/journal/hha/authinfo.htm) says HHA uses EditFlow and requires `hha.cls` for accepted final work. Be prepared for <=~30 HHA-formatted pages; an abstract <=200 words, AMS 2020 MSC and suitable keywords; verify up-to-date details when drafting. The latest `hha.cls` should be obtained from the publisher at the formatting stage, not copied here.

Do not confuse a research notebook, Lean dump, preprint or cover letter with a journal-ready manuscript. Keep third-party references and any disclosure obligations accurate. No copyright transfer, arXiv upload or journal submission occurs without owner instruction.
