# S003 multiparameter coherence frontier — what was and was not solved

**Original target:** arbitrary finite two-parameter diagrams B -> A of finite free integral groups, with strictly nested *three-cover* CW subcomplexes and contractible double intersections. A levelwise mapping-cone construction alone is insufficient: choosing maps inducing commuting matrices does not guarantee the **continuous maps themselves** commute on an incomparable square; a square homotopy is not strict inclusion. In a long grid, choices must satisfy higher coherence. No counterexample to unrestricted finite CW realization was obtained, and no complete universal 2D theorem is claimed.

**Positive test:** any diamond with all objects and arrows split and freely *simultaneously* based, so that all transitions are inclusions of compatible direct summands, and M maps strictly extend on old cells, admits S002-T3's literal wedge attachment scheme, coordinatewise. This is a sufficient subclass, not a general characterization. If a diamond has B00=0 and B10=B01=B11=Z with two identity arrows, the diagram cannot be realized by the one-critical **graph** triple intersections satisfying T10 cap T01=T00 (S003-T7), but it CAN be realized if the two incomparable subcomplexes overlap more than the bottom stage. Neither argument settles unrestricted two-parameter universality.

**Attempted broad route (not promoted to theorem).** Use strict torus homomorphism diagrams to realize arbitrary B diagrams on H1; use classifying spaces BT^a to realize A on H2; natural maps B->A induce maps between suspensions/classifying spaces at the **strict functor** level. Rectify diagrams by an explicit finite-poset bar/homotopy-colimit cofibration model, then apply the three-cover cone construction. Blockers that require a written proof: (i) a strict compatible suspension-arrow map through the bar construction at every stage, (ii) actual CW intersection formulas under rectification, (iii) finite CW truncation simultaneously respecting all diagram maps and preserving H3(Y)=0, and (iv) exact extra homology when the torus T has nonzero H2 (the simple S002 all-other-homology-zero conclusion no longer follows). General homotopy colimit and persistence realization theorems make a naïve "universal" claim a likely prior-art collision.

**Sharper programme candidate.** Characterize when a one-critical, dimension-3 three-cover *bifiltration* can have prescribed integral M as d2 with constant acyclic double-intersection patterns, and identify a computable rank/torsion obstruction involving the latching diagrams H1(T10 cup T01) and H2(U1,10 cup U1,01). An affirmative nontrivial classification would be materially stronger than ordinary persistence realization; current T6/T7 are elementary necessary conditions, not sufficient in general. Test a small complete diamond first, including the case with non-primitive sums of two saturated sublattices.
