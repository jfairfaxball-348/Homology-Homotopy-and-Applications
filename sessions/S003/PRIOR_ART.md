## Exact quantifier comparison and decision

Carlsson–Zomorodian: arbitrary **field** persistence module over a multi-index grid, ordinary PH_n of a finite multi-filtered simplicial complex, n>0. **Not** specifically free integral B->A with a prescribed d2 in a three-member cover, nor necessarily pairwise contractible intersections.

S003-T5: **integral Z** finite free A,B natural arrow on a finite **total chain**, all maps arbitrary, actual nested finite CW three-cover with pairwise contractible intersections, prescribed d2 and kernel/cokernel, dimension growing with chain length. Not directly quoted as Carlsson–Zomorodian Theorem 2, but constructed by classical mapping cylinders/cofibers. **Originality not supported**: an elementary special realization format rather than a demonstrated new significant phenomenon.

S003-T6/T7: literal CW-subcomplex dimension and graph-bifiltration latching obstruction, including a one-critical condition; elementary cellular/MV inequalities likely standard consequences, **not claimed new**. The unrestricted two-parameter integral three-cover question remains open *for this programme*, not a certified open problem in the literature.

**Disposition:** no credible original HHA-level flagship theorem yet; retain restricted one-critical 2D geometry as CONDITIONAL exploration, but **kill publication priority on generic realization alone**. Stage-2 *post-result novelty* cannot pass without a fixed, stronger theorem. No MathSciNet/zbMATH authenticated complete prior-art search; no full primary paper proof inventory completed. Record access limits honestly. No contact or publication.
