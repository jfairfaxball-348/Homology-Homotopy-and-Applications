## Comparative novelty kills — top four seriously compared

- **C02/C03 versus SRC-0009/SRC-0010.** Existing Mayer–Vietoris spectral sequences and extension work already compute persistence from cover complexes and discuss spectral-sequence interleavings. Merely deriving d2, constructing a mapping cone, or noticing a nonsplit extension is **not novel**. Distinct possible research is precise *realisability and rigidity* for actual nested 3-subcomplex covers with integral data. Need inspect full relevant theorem statements beyond available abstract/selected portions before asserting any priority.
- **C10 versus SRC-0014.** Roff, *HHA* 26(1) 2024 105–130, already has a homological Whitney-twist invariance theorem for a broad class of gluings. An invented “general 2-sum” cannot be deemed new without full direct comparison. Reserve only.
- **C08 versus SRC-0012.** Vaupel–Hermansen–Trygsland, *HHA* 27(2) 2025 61–90, Theorem 1.1 establishes diag(section complex)≃X and a section spectral sequence. A collapse/degeneracy theorem requires genuine new hypotheses, not a paraphrase.
- **C05 versus SRC-0007.** Ramras, *DCG* 75 (2026) 871–903, Theorem 2.13 and Props 9.5/9.11 already give refined nerve/fiber comparisons, so a simple fibre-connectivity extension is dead.
