# S111 ADVERSARIAL — objections independently attacked

1. **Graph homeomorphism vs homotopy equivalence.** A nontrivial IA outer class has infinite order; any *fixed finite graph* homeomorphism has finite-order action in Out. It may be represented by a self-map and mapping torus but not as a locally trivial graph bundle. Do not overstate this to prohibit all mapping-torus or abstract homotopy-fibration models.
2. **PL sublevel realisability:** assigning barycentric heights to actual cellular characteristic models creates compatible finite PL sublevels. A partial coned cell below its apex retracts to its boundary; at its apex the whole cell enters. The names/values are user imposed, **not canonical geometry**. Compare after the sublevel/CW inclusion weak-equivalence zigzag, and use ∅ on negative cuts.
3. **False δ=0 stability:** numeric event vectors identical does NOT provide maps or identify different total spaces; δ_event is deliberately a defective proposed metric. Genuine common-domain sup-norm stability is neither refuted nor improved.
4. **Higher Fox/Massey assumptions:** Original Fenn–Sjerve 4.2 applies to Q_w with its single γ3-relator, *not to M_w as a whole*, whose t-b and t-c relators lie in γ2. The map p:M_w→Q_w is the bridge; pullback plus explicitly calculated full indeterminacy is essential. Avoid assuming every M_w defining system canonical or zero indeterminacy.
5. **Massey indeterminacy:** B∪H¹ + H¹∪B equals ZU_b, so ±2U_a cannot be cancelled; a bare Fox coefficient without modding this subgroup would not suffice. Nonzero rational Massey for M_w versus rationally formal M_1 is an unmarked homotopy invariant; the named B,C classes themselves are not canonically unmarked.
6. **CW quotient map:** p sends a,t to basepoint but b,c remain; r_b/r_c maps are contractible in the graph (possible degree-zero fillings), r_a maps to w⁻¹ and its disk maps to the Q_w 2-cell of degree -1. The map is cellular up to harmless subdivisions and induces the claimed cohomology pullback.
