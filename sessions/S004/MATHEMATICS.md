# S004-T11: uniform three-dimensional one-critical integral three-cover transgression

**Theorem (restricted sufficient class, not universal).** Let P={00,10,01,11}. Let A,B:P→f.g. free abelian groups be commuting integral square diagrams and M:B⇒A any natural transformation. Suppose:
(1) A00→A10 and A00→A01 are split injections;
(2) B00→B10,B01 are split injections and λ_B:(B10⊕B01)/B00→B11 is a split injection (T8).
The upper A arrows may be arbitrary noninjective/nonsplit. Then there exist finite, **dimension ≤3** CW Xp with three literally nested CW subcomplexes U1,p,U2,p,U3,p covering Xp, such that each named U, each intersection and X obeys literal meet T10∩T01=T00 (replace T by each space), all pair intersections and U2,U3 are contractible, the triple intersection T_p is a connected graph with reduced H1=B_p, and U1 has reduced H2=A_p and no other reduced homology. The integral 3-cover MV spectral-sequence d₂:H1(T)→H2(U1) is exactly the given M naturally over the entire square; reduced H3(X)=ker M and H2(X)=coker M, with other reduced homology zero. Not claimed necessary and sufficient for all squares; only ordinary (possibly nonregular) finite CW is asserted.

**Step 1, graph T.** By T8 choose nested roses T00⊂T10,T01⊂T11, with T10∩T01=T00. The upper top includes their literal union plus *new independent circles*. Orient and identify H1 with B as a commuting diagram including zero rank.

**Step 2, 3D Y with arbitrary upper A arrows.** Choose compatible bases extending A00 to A10,A01. Construct Y00 as a wedge of 2-spheres and Y10,Y01 by adding disjoint wedge summands to the common Y00. Their union V_Y=Y10∪Y01 has H2=(A10⊕A01)/A00=:D_A. The commuting square yields an arbitrary λ_A:D_A→A11. Let E=∨^(rank A11)S². Choose based finite cellular maps from each 2-sphere of V_Y to E representing λ_A (pinch and signed degree maps), yielding a *single* h:V_Y→E. Set Y11=M_h, the ordinary mapping cylinder, retaining domain V_Y as an actual CW subcomplex. Y11 has dim≤3, retracts onto E, and contains Y10,Y01 intersecting at Y00, with EXACT prescribed top homology arrows even when nonprimitive, noninjective or zero.

**Step 3, literal maps f.** Fix cone endpoints u,v and form the unreduced suspension S_p=C_uT_p∪_(T_p)C_vT_p. These 2-dimensional complexes are nested with S10∩S01=S00; the suspension of the common wedge vertex is a contractible spine I. Quotienting I yields a compatible wedge of one S² for each rose edge. Choose based cellular maps f00:S00→Y00, f10:S10→Y10, f01:S01→Y01 representing M, taking I to the common basepoint. On old generators the side maps are the *same* f00, because both A and B lower maps are coordinate inclusions and M naturality forces zero new coordinates in old columns. Thus f10 and f01 agree pointwise on S00, and glue to f_V:S10∪S01→Y11. The new top independent T11 rose edges contribute independent suspended S² summands in S11; map each such summand into simply connected Y11 with the prescribed M11 column. Hurewicz π2(Y11)=H2(Y11) since Y11≃∨S², so all integral columns have finite based cellular representatives. The previous columns already induce M11 by naturality. This defines f11 extending both side maps *literally*, hence every arrow and the square commute as actual restrictions, not only up to homotopy.

**Step 4, literal labelled covers and dimension.** Write P_p=C_uT_p, Q_p=C_vT_p, S_p=P_p∪Q_p and W_p=C_w S_p with fixed third cone vertex w. Put U1,p=M_(f_p), U2,p=C_wP_p, U3,p=C_wQ_p, X_p=M_(f_p)∪_(S_p) W_p retaining the suspension S_p at the domain end of the mapping cylinder. Fixed product/cone CW structures ensure all grade maps are literal inclusions and every labelled space meets at grade 00: all additional cells on incomparable sides are distinct, while Y10∩Y01=Y00 and S10∩S01=S00. The actual intersection identities are
U1∩U2=P, U1∩U3=Q, U2∩U3=C_wT, U1∩U2∩U3=T.
All double overlaps are contractible; U2,U3 are cones. Since dim T≤1, dim S≤2, dim Y≤3, both M_f and C_w S have dimension at most 3, so **dim X≤3**, no hidden 4D step.

**Step 5, d₂ naturality.** X=M_f∪_S CS is the (ordinary cellular) homotopy cofiber of f. Its reduced natural long exact sequence, H2(S)=H1(T)=B and H2(Y)=A, with all other reduced homology of S,Y zero, gives 0→H3(X)→B→^M A→H2(X)→0, with all other reduced homology zero. The 3-cover Cech-Mayer–Vietoris E² has only positive groups E²_(2,1)=H1(T) and E²_(0,2)=H2(U1). Its d₂ is the standard two-step transgression H1(T)≅H2(P∪Q=S)→H2(U1), equal to f_* up to a globally coherent orientation choice (S002-T1). Strict maps make all identifications commute over P. This proves the claims. QED.

**Integral torsion / nonsplit test.** Take A00=B00=0; A10=A01=B10=B01=Z; A11=B11=Z². Upper B maps are columns (1,0),(0,1), so B meets T8. Upper A columns are (1,0),(1,2), an index-two combined nonprimitive image. Let M10=[2], M01=[1], M11=[[2,1],[0,2]]. Naturality: (1,0)·2=(2,0) and (1,2)·1=(1,2). At 10 coker=Z/2, at 01 coker=0; at 11 relations 2e1=0, e1+2e2=0 imply e1=-2e2, 4e2=0 and coker=Z/4, ker=0. T11 geometrically realizes this **in total dimension 3** with literal one-critical intersections. Symbolic integral verification, not a numerical search.

**Gaps and adversarial boundary.** Arbitrary nonprimitive λ_B makes graph T impossible; T9 makes a 2D T but the present suspension/cone method then usually has dimension four, NOT a general dimension-three impossibility. Split lower A is sufficient but its necessity is not shown. Classical mapping cylinder/cofiber arguments may eliminate novelty; no original HHA-quality claim or Lean certification follows.
