## 1. All-dimensional T28/T29 proof audit

For n>=2 put B_n=2^[n], bottom b=empty set, top t=[n]. Stalks are finitely generated free abelian groups; arrows are all composites of the cube edges. The LLO Definition 3 induction gives precisely all principal upsets P_u and principal downsets I_v, identifying P_b=I_t, hence 2^(n+1)-1 support types (and not arbitrary mixed rectangles). Base n=2 has 4+4-1=7; n=3 has 15; n=4 has 31. Induced blocks simply duplicate a lower-dimensional upset/downset along an entire coordinate; no extra Boolean blocks.

Fix v<t, missing axes J=[n]\\v. Let K_v=intersection of kernels of M(v->v+e_j), j in J, and L_v=intersection of kernels M(b->e_j), j in J. For |J|>=2 these are top homology of |J|-face Koszul complexes; the preceding H_{|J|-1} is torsionfree/zero by the all-field middle-exact conditions, so UCT identifies K_v tensor Fp with the ACTUAL field joint kernel, likewise L_v. If |J|=1, the corresponding two-term complex needs torsionfree generating-edge cokernel for kernel base change. Over Q kernel commute with extension automatically. A scalar multiplication-by-m generating edge (m>1) is the failure witness if edge purity is removed.

The bottom-to-v arrow restricts to a_v:L_v->K_v. For field P_u, K_v=0; for I_w, the K_v contribution is one iff w=v, while the L_v contribution is one iff w<=v. Consequently the field a_v is onto for Q and for every Fp. The finitely generated coker of a_v has zero rational rank and zero modulo every prime, so vanishes. K_v is saturated as kernel of the map M(v)->direct sum M(v+e_j), hence free and a_v has a Z-linear section s. A retraction q:M(v)->K_v exists. Define a natural I_v(K_v) injection i_x=M(b->x)s when x<=v, zero outside, and retraction r_x=q M(x->v) when x<=v, zero outside. An outgoing crossing edge must increase some j in J and kills s(L_v); no arrows go from outside into the downset. Thus both are natural and r i=id at ALL x<=v. Remove full death coefficients, repeat at every v<t. All subsequent summands and complements remain edge-pure and field-block-split; field Krull–Schmidt removes exactly I_v. Joint kernels cannot reappear under direct-summand passage.

The residual has only field principal upsets. For arbitrary finite poset P, the canonical latching map at v is lambda_v:colim_{x<v}F(x)->F(v), not the naive sum of incoming images. In a linear extension, after splitting old stalks, the predecessor colimit is canonically direct sum of earlier birth coefficient groups C_u (u<v); this is free. Tensor preserves colimits, and field representables have split-injective lambda. Rational and every prime injectivity of lambda forces its kernel zero and integral cokernel torsionfree by Smith/UCT, allowing a free complement C_v. That inductively constructs ONE natural simultaneous integral decomposition. This is T29 (elementary projective-latching descent; highly classically colliding). The T28 integral finite-face criterion is exactly primitive edges, each k>=2 face H_i=0 for 1<=i<=k-1 and H_0 torsionfree; UCT translates these to all-field middle exactness, then LLO Theorem14 supplies the source field blocks.

**Adversarial edge cases.** n=2 one full 2-face and four 1-edges; n=3 all six square faces PLUS full cube; n=4 all 2-faces, 3-faces and full 4-face, not only squares. M=0 throughout gives zero coefficient groups. P_b full support has K_v=0 for v<t and survives once; I_b isolated bottom has K_b=M(b), peels without any illicit division; P_t only top has latching zero. Composite cokernels become pure after splitting (or directly from block form), not assumed. This is a symbolic universal proof audit, **not** exhaustive integer-matrix testing or independent formal certification. No counterexample satisfying all T28 hypotheses was constructed.

## 2. T32 — exact derived colimits on a Boolean cube missing its top (new-to-programme calculation)

Let P=B_n\\{t}, n>=2, and A:P->Ab be ANY diagram of abelian groups (not necessarily free, projective or block-decomposable). Write A_S=A(S). Define the finite *truncated cubical* chain complex
C_j(A)= direct_sum_{S subsetneq [n], |S|=n-1-j} A_S  (0<=j<=n-1),
with differential the alternating sum of the arrow maps A_S->A_{S union {i}}, for i not in S, to the next larger subsets (omit t). Signs are the standard cubical/Koszul incidence signs.

**Claim T32:** naturally in A, H_j(C(A)) = L_j colim_P A for every j>=0. In particular
L_{n-1} colim_P A = intersection_{i=1}^n ker[A_b->A_{e_i}]
for ANY A, including nonfree stalks.

**Detailed proof.** C_0=direct sum over all facets S of size n-1. Every element of a lower vertex reaches a facet, and all relations in the colimit are generated by the relations between two such face images at a common immediate lower coface, so coker(C_1->C_0) is the categorical colimit. For n=2, C_1=A_b and C_0=A_{e1} direct-sum A_{e2}; for n>2 the remaining relations follow from commutative squares and expansion along saturated chains. More structurally: on P, the functors Q^S=Z Hom_P(S,-) (S<t) form projective generators of Ab^P. The chain C_j(Q^S) is the cellular chain complex of the full simplex on the coordinates absent from S, with its terminal/top face removed only when S=b. Since S<t, this complex is exact in positive degrees and H_0=Z, as can be verified by the contraction toward any facet containing S; for S=b, it is the augmented boundary complex with exact positive homology and H_0=Z. [Care: the formula is with Q^S as a diagram, not the I_b skyscraper.] Thus C is an exact-in-degree evaluation construction giving a projective (acyclic-on-generators) resolution of the colimit functor, and its homology computes the left-derived colimits. Alternatively the standard simplicial-replacement (Roos) complex collapses by elementary Boolean-chain subdivisions onto this cellular cubical complex, naturally up to chain homotopy. Highest degree has only A_b, and its differential consists exactly of the n outgoing maps with signs; hence the joint-kernel formula.

**Important consistency check:** for the representable Q^b, C has no positive-degree homology despite containing A_b=Z, because outgoing maps are identities. For the bottom skyscraper I_b, the outgoing maps are zero, so L_{n-1}=Z. For any downset I_v (b<v<t), the missing-coordinate proper nonempty set J=[n]\\v yields a full augmented Cech/Koszul resolution by representables of the complement of the downset: L_j colim_P I_v=0 for all j. For any upset P_u restricted to P, colim=Z if u<t and higher derived colimits zero; P_t restricts to zero. This checks the formula on every T28 block.

The full cube Koszul complex K(M) has the same chain terms with the additional degree-zero M_t; C_j(M|P)=K_{j+1}(M), so L_j colim_P M = H_{j+1}(K(M)) for every j>=1. For j=0, colim is the quotient C_0/im(d_2); the canonical map lambda_t:colim_P M->M_t has kernel H_1(K(M)) and cokernel H_0(K(M)). These identifications are natural for all abelian-valued cube diagrams.

**Corollary for T28 modules.** If M satisfies T28, H_i(K)=0 for 1<=i<=n-1 and H_0(K) is free. Therefore L_j colim_P M=0 for 1<=j<=n-2, L_{n-1}=K_b, lambda_t is injective with free cokernel. In a T28 block decomposition L_{n-1} has rank exactly d_b, so the most extreme global bottom death multiplicity is a canonical functorial derived-colimit rank. This part needs no chosen barcode isomorphism. Other d_v can be recovered by applying analogous intervals/restricted faces when the indexing is appropriate.

## 3. T33 — sparse homotopy-colimit homology spectral sequence

Let X:P->Top be a diagram of CW complexes (or simplicial sets), with homotopy colimit formed via the standard simplicial replacement, and suppose for every q>=0 the **integral** homology diagram H_q(X(-);Z), extended to a full Boolean cube by a compatible top X_t, satisfies T28's exact integer hypotheses. Assume bounded-below homology; finite P gives finite filtration and standard strong convergence of the first-quadrant Bousfield–Kan homology spectral sequence
E^2_{p,q}=L_p colim_P H_q(X;Z) ==> H_{p+q}(hocolim_P X;Z).
Then E2 has nonzero terms only in columns p=0 and p=n-1, with E2_{n-1,q} naturally the joint bottom outgoing kernel of H_q, a free group of rank d_b(q); E2_{0,q} is the (free) saturated image of the top-latching map in H_q(X_t;Z). In the homological convention d_r:(p,q)->(p-r,q+r-1), **for n>=3** the only potentially nonzero differential is d_{n-1} from (n-1,q) to (0,q+n-2); for n=2 the E2 page collapses, though extensions may remain in abutment. No assertion of vanishing of d_{n-1}, canonical splitting of E_infty or absence of integral extensions. The BK spectral sequence itself is classical; this is an explicit sparse consequence of T28+T32, not a novelty or HHA application certificate.

**HHA significance boundary:** this reaches derived colimits and topological homotopy colimits beyond the elementary circle/disk examples, but it currently gives a constrained spectral-sequence *shape*, not an exhibited nonzero d_{n-1}, new manifold/CW application, or unexpected collapse. The chain-level identification is near-standard homological algebra. HHA editorial fit remains INSUFFICIENTLY DEMONSTRATED.

## 4. Longer-chain grid front: no full mixed-grid conclusion

In dimension 2, G={0,1,2}^2, genuine field/integral induced band B={1}x{0,1,2} (identity maps inside) has B(0,0)=0 while its outgoing horizontal terminal kernel K_(1,2)=Z. Already 2D global-bottom death lifting 0->Z fails. They DO NOT disprove global integral block splitting.

A slice-first approach works on one isolated B by using (1,0,0) as band bottom; for a general diagram multiple overlapping middle bands produce incoming latching images and outgoing kernel filtrations at different slices, and choosing saturated complements separately does NOT establish commuting idempotents or natural retractions. No complete compatible filtration/order, no mixed-grid iff proof, and NO all-hypotheses matrix counterexample were obtained. T30/T31 remain genuine but strict extremal subclasses. A PID/finite-poset extension of T29 is possible with every finitely generated projective coefficient free; on general Dedekind domains projective coefficient ideals need not be free. This is a boundary observation, NOT an originality claim or solved grid problem.
