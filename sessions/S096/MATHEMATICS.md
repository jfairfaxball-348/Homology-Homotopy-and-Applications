## T228. Exact CP2 / wedge phase law in ordinary unpointed Top^R

Fix **any** real a<b<c<d. Define F_t, G_t as literally nested CW inclusions: empty for t<a; S² for a≤t<b; F=CP²=S²∪_η e⁴, G=S²∨S⁴ for b≤t<c; F=CP²∨S⁵, G=S²∨S⁴∨S⁵ for c≤t<d; a one-point space for t≥d. All maps into the last point are collapse maps; earlier maps are canonical inclusions. The diagrams use unpointed Top and ordinary objectwise weak homotopy equivalences (not 2-local or equivariant equivalences). At every time their graded integral homology and all persistence structure maps are naturally isomorphic.

**Exact equality, internally proved:**
 d_HI(F,G) = d_IHC(F,G) = min{ d−b, (d−a)/2 }.
Here IHC is interleaving in (Ho Top)^R; HI is the homotopy-interleaving metric allowing arbitrary WHOLE real-diagram objectwise-WE replacements of BOTH lanes. Consequently neither this example nor its homology-blindness establishes a strict HI>IHC difference.

*Lower bound: all real ε, both identities.* Suppose ε<d−b and 2ε<d−a. Any putative IHC ε-interleaving has a natural component α_b:F_b=CP²→G_(b+ε), whose target is either S²∨S⁴ or S²∨S⁴∨S⁵ because b≤b+ε<d. Write h∈H²(CP²;Z), h² a generator of H⁴(CP²;Z), and u the S² generator on the target, where u²=0. The induced α_b^*(u)=k h satisfies 0=α_b^*(u²)=k² h², hence k=0. Thus α_b is zero on H₂(Z). Since ε<d−b entails a+ε<b+ε<d, the shifted G structure map from a+ε to b+ε is an isomorphism on H₂, as is F_a→F_b. Naturality of α in (Ho Top)^R forces α_a to induce zero H₂. But a+2ε<d means F_a→F_(a+2ε) is an H₂-isomorphism, contradicting the interleaving identity β_(a+ε) α_a=F_(a→a+2ε). This rules out every ε strictly below BOTH d−b and (d−a)/2. Passing from genuine whole-diagram HI to IHC through Ho Top shows the same lower bound for HI, without assuming chosen replacement types.

*Upper bound at ε=d−b: explicit STRICT transformations.* On source times a≤t<b, map the common S² into the canonical S² subcomplex at shifted target time t+ε whenever it is alive, and otherwise collapse to point. For t≥b the shifted target is already the terminal point (because b+ε=d), so use collapse; for t<a use the unique map from empty. Do this symmetrically in both lanes. Each real naturality square commutes ON THE NOSE: both maps agree with literal inclusions when the shifted target has not died; otherwise all maps collapse; the only divergent stage begins at b but its shifted target is dead. For each t≥b, t+2ε≥d, making the 2ε structure maps and composites the same collapse. If a≤t<b and t+2ε<d, then t+ε<b (using ε=d−b), so the intermediate source on the opposite lane is still the identical S² and the 2ε composition is exactly the canonical structure map. At t+2ε=d equality is into the terminal point. All t<a and t≥d also satisfy identities uniquely. No requirement for a CP²→wedge map of nonzero degree occurs.

*Upper bound at ε=(d−a)/2:* use literal constant basepoint-preserving transformations after the first nonempty stage a and unique maps from empty beforehand. For every t≥a, t+2ε≥d, hence both 2ε compositions are exactly the terminal collapses; for t<a all identities are unique maps from empty. Choose common CW basepoints respected by all inclusions to obtain strict naturality. These exhibit strict interleavings of the original diagrams, hence HI≤min of the two bounds, proving equality. Every equality boundary, t<0, t=a,b,c,d, and all other real t is included. The c parameter is mathematically inert: this formula is a classical attaching obstruction coupled to a death clock, not a new higher multiwindow effect.

## T229. Stable prime-order attaching classes: elementary generalisation, not an originality certificate

Let n≥4 and m>n+1 be integers within the Freudenthal stable range m−1≤2n−2. Choose a NONZERO element α∈π_(m−1)(S^n) of exact prime order p. Put X=S^n∪_α e^m and Y=S^n∨S^m, and append a common freely wedged S^(m+1) at c. Use the identical four-stage all-real filtration defined in T228, with CP² replaced by X and the wedge by Y and S² by S^n.

**Claim (internal):** d_HI^Top(Fα,Gα)=d_IHC^Top(Fα,Gα)=min{d−b,(d−a)/2}, whereas all ordinary graded persistent homology diagrams with integral coefficients agree exactly.

*Proof of degree obstruction.* If h:X→Y∨S^(m+1) induced degree k on H_n, composing with the canonical retraction Y∨S^(m+1)→S^n produces an extension of a degree-k map S^n→S^n across the attaching α. Its restriction to the attaching sphere is null, so (degree k)_* α=0. In the stated stable range degree-k self-maps act as multiplication by k on the corresponding stable stem. Since α has order p, p divides k. Therefore h acts by zero on H_n(−;F_p). In the lower-bound argument of T228 replace the cup-square zero on H₂(Z) by this mod-p degree obstruction; identical H_n bar naturality and the a-stage 2ε identity force ε≥min{d−b,(d−a)/2}. The exact two strict upper interleavings require only the shared initial S^n subcomplex, and their proofs are unchanged. Homology matches because the m-cell differential has no (m−1)-cell target and all map-induced homology maps in degrees n,m,m+1 are canonical inclusions. This is an exact *ordinary unpointed* whole-real two-lane assertion, but comes directly from stable attaching-class nonextension plus clock maps and thus fails the nonroutine novelty requirement.

## T230. Secondary eta² example invisible to ordinary homology, cup and *primary* Steenrod action

Take n≥5, m=n+3, α=η_n²∈π_(n+2)(S^n)=π_2^S≅Z/2. Its nontriviality/order and stable range are standard stable homotopy facts (2026 ICM original table, SRC-0228). The T229 formula holds with p=2. At stage b the two lanes have mod-2 cohomology only in degrees 0,n,n+3; since n≥5 there is no nonzero positive cup product by degree. The only conceivable primary Steenrod square hitting the top cell from H^n is Sq³, but Sq³=Sq¹Sq² and H^(n+2)=0, so Sq³ vanishes. Other squares have no target. At stage c the freely wedged S^(n+4) adds a class without altering Sq actions on the original summand, by the wedge retraction and naturality. At odd primes no degree-3 reduced power exists, and the wedge cell is likewise split; Bocksteins vanish because all integral homology is torsionfree. Thus the entire all-real persistent graded cohomology, its cup multiplication and PRIMARY Steenrod-module diagrams on both lanes are isomorphic, yet ordinary Top homotopy interleaving has strictly positive exact distance min{d−b,(d−a)/2}. A secondary cohomology operation detecting η² is classical; the present session does not construct its full choice/indeterminacy functor as a stable persistent invariant. This example defeats the claim "cup and primary operations exhaust homotopical persistence," but is NOT by itself HHA-original secondary persistence theory.
