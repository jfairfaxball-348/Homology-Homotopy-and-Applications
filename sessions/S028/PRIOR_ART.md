# S028 primary-source audit and priority decision

Lanari–Scoccola original final-to-appear arXiv2010.05378v3 PDF retrieved in full (25 pages), compared with AGT23(2023)803–832 metadata. Selected §3.3 Proposition3.14/Lemma3.15 and their proof on manuscript page15 were directly read. They already extend inequivalent coherent squares with identical Ho(Top) diagrams to R², obtaining dHC=0 and a positive dHI plateau gap. Their TheoremB, §5 proof and Remark5.14 concern a comparison morphism in Ho(S^(R^m)) inducing all homotopy-group interleavings; abstract isomorphic group diagrams do not supply that morphism. This is the direct mechanism match that previous metadata-only audits missed. The journal-final PDF endpoint remained inaccessible; the actual proof source is explicitly arXivv3, not an invented publisher retrieval. No claim that their paper printed our exact Euclidean matrices.

BL2017 original author PDF sections3,7,8 inspected again: coherent interleavings and their weak-equivalence replacements are prior art. We retain both identities in our plateau proof. The already cited Example8.5 has different single-parameter hypotheses and is not represented as the exact finite pair.

Munson–Volić original author-hosted full PDF was successfully downloaded this time (45,053,013 bytes). Chapter3 §§3.8 and selected §3.9 proof passages read, printed pages168–175; pages168 and171 were also rendered and visually inspected. Correction of earlier indexed-excerpt attribution: Definition3.8.1 defines total cofiber; Definition3.8.8 defines the homology of a square, not total cofiber. Proposition3.8.2 describes iterated cofibers; Proposition3.8.9 the square LES. The constructions are classical. The entire book was not read. Full PDF bytes/hashes are recorded in SOURCE_RETRIEVAL.json, not redistributed.

Mémoli–Stefanou–Zhou original JACT8(2024)93–148 HTML: Theorem2 and its proof, Lemma3.5 and proof inspected; this is general weak-homotopy invariant stability for their persistent-space setting. It is a precedent, not a literal theorem about our integral mixed-square coefficient. The paper's stated field/one-filtration scope is preserved.

Madahar–Sarkaria original author Hopf.pdf §§2,3,5 reread. It supplies the 12→4 simplicial generator and CP² antecedent. Musin–Shamazov, Computing the Hopf invariant, arXiv2512.03201v1 (2 December2025), original full HTML §§2–3 lemmas and algorithm inspected. Solving a pullback primitive and evaluating a source AW Hopf pairing is already a published-preprint algorithm, not our novelty. Our finite output goes further in checking a selected actual relative total-cofiber chain diagonal, without claiming a novel algorithm.

Blaser–Brun–Gardaa–Salbu JACT2026 original HTML §6.3 Proposition6.7 and proof reread. Oudot–Waas SoCG2026 original proceedings HTML Theorem1, Definition28 and the function-Rips comparison discussion inspected (not full proof audit). These provide existing geometric persistent-homotopy background, not a strict R5 solution.

Nonexhaustive current-source searches for Hopf/multiparameter/cofiber/cup/coherent variants were run with both search engines. Sparse and noisy results are not novelty evidence. No independent exact theorem matching every finite Euclidean and integer quantifier was located, and no genuinely nonroutine new application was established. The tested all-field-versus-integer coefficient distinction is classical arithmetic of cone rings.

Decision: retire the Hopf/coherent-square obstruction as the current HHA flagship, preserve T46–T64 as correct benchmarks where already verified. This is a target-selection judgment supported by a direct source match; it is not a proof that every concrete certificate has earlier literal publication. First novelty Gate2 remains NOT_STARTED, not passed.
