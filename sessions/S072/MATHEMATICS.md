## 1. Model and definitions: do not identify two different categories

Fix the stable proper simplicial model category Sp of symmetric spectra in pointed simplicial sets, with all mapping spaces derived using cofibrant source and fibrant target; replace objects if needed. Stable weak equivalences, zero maps, and the projective model structure on any small diagram category are used throughout. Let [4] denote the free linear category 0→1→2→3→4 (four arrows, five objects), **not** a generic commutative square. Define
 h: Ho(Sp^[4]) → Ho(Sp)^[4]
by applying objectwise localization. This functor retains less morphism information; it is not asserted faithful.

A Ho(Sp)^[4] chain X=(A0 -a0→ A1 -a1→ A2 -a2→ A3 -a3→ A4) is **strict-double-zero rectifiable**, abbreviated R4(X), if there is a strict chain C in Sp and an isomorphism h(C)≅X in Ho(Sp)^[4], with its three strict double composites d1d0, d2d1, d3d2 equal to the literal zero morphisms. This is a well-typed *predicate*, not a newly named Kochman bracket. It is invariant under **any** isomorphism X≅Y in Ho(Sp)^[4] simply by composition of isomorphisms; no spurious equality of diagram homotopy categories is used.

Define the complete general BBG fourfold bracket B4(X) via BBG 2016 Example 2.17, Def4.27–4.31 for the pointed simplicial enrichment of a small full subcategory of cofibrant-fibrant spectra containing the five objects, with core S^0, 0th-order homotopy chain of four edges and all higher compatible choices. It takes values in [Σ²A0,A4] (after stable suspension identifications), perhaps empty when no coherent second-order defining system exists. No bracket value is a single arbitrarily preferred nullhomotopy: it is the *complete union* over all choices. The path-space model has three 1-cube nullhomotopies H02,H13,H24, two compatible 2-cube fillers H03,H14, and the three boundary contributions H03 followed by d3, H02 paired with H24, and d0 followed by H14; the assembled 2-sphere is the value. Here order/composition sign conventions may reverse but zero-membership is unambiguous in the stated BBG model. This is BBG's construction, NOT by declaration Kochman's Def2.2.1 for arbitrary spectra.

## 2. T188 — finite-chain lifting under pointwise homotopy-chain isomorphism

**Theorem S072-T188.** The canonical h for a finite free linear category [n] is full and conservative and essentially surjective. Thus for any strict X,Y in Sp^[n], an arbitrary isomorphism h(X)≅h(Y) can be represented by a zigzag of **whole-diagram objectwise weak equivalences** in Sp^[n]. It is *not* necessarily an equality or direct map without replacements.

**Proof.** Use the projective (equivalently on a direct finite linear order, Reedy-cofibrant) replacement QX→X, so the latching/edge maps QX_{i−1}→QX_i are cofibrations and all QX_i are cofibrant. Choose an objectwise fibrant diagram RY representing Y (projective fibrant replacement). A natural transformation in Ho(Sp)^[n] gives classes [v_i]:QX_i→RY_i with [v_i]∘[q_i]=[r_i]∘[v_{i−1}]. After v_{i−1} is already chosen strictly, choose any representative v_i of the desired class. The two restrictions to QX_{i−1} are homotopic because their classes agree and cofibrancy/fibrancy make the mapping-space homotopy relation correct. The restriction map Map(QX_i,RY_i)→Map(QX_{i−1},RY_i) is a Kan fibration by SM7 because q_i is a cofibration and RY_i fibrant. Lift the path between the restrictions starting at v_i to a path in Map(QX_i,RY_i); its endpoint v_i' has **literal** equality v_i' q_i=r_i v_{i−1} and represents [v_i]. Induct from i=0 to n. This gives a strict natural v:QX→RY mapping to the prescribed h-arrow. If each [v_i] is an isomorphism, all v_i are stable weak equivalences (Whitehead for cofibrant-fibrant representatives), and the zigzag X←QX→RY←Y is whole-diagram objectwise WE. Essential surjectivity is obtained by selecting strict representatives of free edges and composing, with model replacements. This is the finite free-chain instance of Lanari–Scoccola Lemma 3.4 (their theorem for Z). No commutative square with relations is claimed to have this same lifting property. QED.

**Quantifiers:** arbitrary five-object chain X and arbitrary pointwise chain isomorphism h(X)≅h(Y), with inverse arrow classes as required; n finite; arbitrary directions of whole WEs in resulting zigzag.

## 3. T189 — explicit nonfaithfulness (a genuine finite coherent counterexample)

**Theorem S072-T189.** h:Ho(Sp^[1])→Ho(Sp)^[1] is not faithful, hence neither is the analogous functor on [4].

**Proof.** Let S be a cofibrant-fibrant sphere-spectrum model (use a cofibrant replacement where required), CS a functorial reduced cone with S↪CS a cofibration and CS stably contractible, and take the cofibrant arrow X=(S→CS), which is weak equivalent as an arrow to (S→0). Take Y=(0→ΣS) with a fibrant target replacement. The derived simplicial mapping space from X to Y is the homotopy pullback of
 Map(S,0)=* → Map(S,ΣS) ← Map(CS,ΣS)≃* .
It is thus weak equivalent to Ω Map(S,ΣS), and its π0 is π1 Map(S,ΣS)=[ΣS,ΣS]≅Z. Explicit representatives are maps CS/S≃ΣS→ΣS of different degrees, postcomposed with the quotient CS→CS/S; all their vertex components become zero in Ho(Sp) since CS≃0 and the other target is zero. Therefore infinitely many distinct morphisms in the diagram homotopy category map to the **same** zero pair of vertexwise homotopy classes. Extend X,Y by zero objects to a five-vertex free chain to obtain the [4] example. This does not contradict T188: fullness and detection of isomorphisms are weaker than faithfulness. QED.

**Hostile interpretation:** A coherent natural transformation cannot be replaced by only its vertex classes when claiming an equality in Ho(Sp^[4]); conversely, an *isomorphism* of five-object pointwise chains can still be lifted to a whole WE via T188.

## 4. T190 — general BBG zero-membership is a pointwise-chain invariant

**Theorem S072-T190.** For any two BBG-admissible four-edge homotopy chains X,Y in Sp with adjacent classes composing to zero, any isomorphism u:X≅Y in Ho(Sp)^[4] induces a bijection of complete BBG fourfold bracket values via the induced equivalences of source and target derived mapping spectra (up to suspension orientation); in particular
 0∈B4(X) ⇔ 0∈B4(Y).
If R4(X) then 0∈B4(X).

**Proof/dependency ledger.** First lift u to a natural whole-diagram zigzag of objectwise WEs using T188; replace the objects in that zigzag by cofibrant-fibrant models. For each natural objectwise WE v:Q→C, form the small full simplicial subcategory D whose objects include both the five source and five target objects (and any necessary strict intermediate cofibrant-fibrant replacements). The two full five-object subcategories K_Q,K_C include in D as Dwyer–Kan equivalences: on mapping simplicial sets the inclusions are identities for their pairs, and every omitted object is equivalent in π0D to its corresponding included object via v_i. The two chains become equivalent 0th-order homotopy chain complexes in D, since v_{i+1}d_i=d_i'v_i **strictly** for this chosen natural map. By BBG Theorem 4.21 (printed p17–19), each DK equivalence induces a bijection between the **complete** higher Toda bracket sets; BBG Definition 4.31 and Remark 4.32 make the bracket independent of the choice of homotopy chain model. Pulling back along all legs of the zigzag gives the asserted bijection, including empty-set and zero-membership cases. No assumption that h is faithful is involved. For literal zero doubles in C choose each three primary nullhomotopies and both higher compatible fillers to be the constant zero maps/cubes. All three final boundary terms are constant zero and the assembled S² map is zero, so 0∈B4(C), and transport back to X. QED.

**Scope caution:** This proves invariance **of the BBG-defined higher bracket in the specified simplicial enriched model**, assuming BBG Theorem4.21's path-model/core/admissibility hypotheses (which are met by the usual simplicial enrichments after cofibrant-fibrant replacement). This does NOT itself prove the BBG bracket equals every classical fourfold variant including the specific one Xu uses.

## 5. T191 — complete all-whole-WE spectral HI obstruction, with an honest antecedent

Let F_X:R→Sp be zero for negative times, equal to A_i on [i,i+1) for i=0,...,4, and zero after time 5, using a strict representative of X; let O be the identically zero real diagram. Define d_HI by a strict ε-interleaving on *some arbitrary whole-diagram objectwise-WE zigzag replacements of F_X AND O*; define d_IHC in Ho(Sp^R), never Ho(Sp)^R.

**Theorem S072-T191.** If B4(X) is defined as above and 0∉B4(X), then d_HI(F_X,O)≥5/4. More generally, if not R4(X), the same lower bound follows without any bracket comparison.

**Proof.** Assume an ε-interleaving for some ε<5/4 after arbitrary entire WEs of both lanes. Independently review S071-T187: its single projectively cofibrant replacement of the full real shoelace diagram, natural pushout along a WE from the acyclic second lane and left properness, and exact stage sampling produce a strict five-vertex chain C whose three length-two composites are **literal zero**, with h(C)≅X in Ho(Sp)^[4]. This is R4(X), and by T190 implies 0∈B4(X), contrary to the hypothesis. No replacement of an acyclic but nonzero second lane by strict zero without a whole natural map is made. No unstated property of a particular representative is required. As no ε<5/4 can work, the infimum d_HI is ≥5/4. QED.

**Independently checked T187 premise:** projectively cofibrant cells of the shoelace poset are covariant representables times generating cofibrations, hence any comparable Q(x)→Q(y) is a cofibration; the crossing map from the zero lane is WE into 0; left properness yields one natural Q_F→C WE; the 2ε arrows vanish after pushout; all real times, negative times, and the half-open endpoints are treated by the original theorem. This is an independent step audit, not a claim of machine verification or complete external referee certification. The 5/4 endpoint is not excluded, nor is exact d_HI computed.
