## T286. Explicit finite CW *inclusion* filtrations and exact natural integral persistence

Let B=F(a,b,c); choose any word w∈[F(b,c),F(b,c)]. Define φ_w(a)=a w, φ_w(b)=b and φ_w(c)=c. Its inverse replaces w by w^(-1), since φ_w fixes w. A pointed graph map on the three-loop rose R_3 realizing φ_w is a cellular homotopy equivalence; its mapping torus M_w is finite 2-dimensional CW with one vertex, four oriented 1-cells a,b,c,t, and three 2-cells with boundary words
r_b=t b t^(-1)b^(-1), r_c=t c t^(-1)c^(-1), r_a=t a t^(-1)(a w)^(-1).
The CW presentation is the standard mapping-torus presentation; because the graph map is a homotopy equivalence its π1 is B⋊_{φ_w} Z. For w=1 this is R_3×S^1 up to CW homotopy.

Define K_{w,0}={v}; K_{w,1}=R_4; K_{w,2}=R_4∪e_b²; K_{w,3}=K_{w,2}∪e_c²; K_{w,4}=K_{w,3}∪e_a²=M_w. All are literal finite CW subcomplex inclusions. Put k(u)=0 for u<0, k(u)=1 for 0≤u<1, k(u)=2 for 1≤u<2, k(u)=3 for 2≤u<3, k(u)=4 for u≥3; F_w(u)=K_{w,k(u)}. Thus there are four genuine changes after the initial vertex, not one isolated pulse, with nontrivial effects on H1 or H2 at u=0,1,2,3. For an R² variant set F_w(s,t)=K_{w,k(min(s,t))}; the finite CW inclusions are functorial and both coordinates are essential at the diagonal threshold.

Cellular ∂1=0 (one vertex). Each attaching word has zero exponent sum in a,b,c,t: the defining r_b/r_c are commutators, and exponent(w)=0 in b,c. Hence ∂2=0 for all stages, for every such w. In bases given by the same formal cell names: H0(K_{w,j};Z)=Z; H1=0 at j0 and Z⁴ for j≥1; H2=0 for j≤1 and Z^{j−1} for j=2,3,4; all H_i≥3 vanish. Inclusion maps act by the literal coordinate inclusions Z^m→Z^{m+1} and the H1 identity after stage1. This identifies the complete natural integral persistent homology functors F_w and F_1, not just their Betti barcodes. Same proof for arbitrary commutative coefficient group via free integral chains; no assertion about local coefficients.

## T287. Centers, arbitrary WE zigzags and exact infinite HI for these filtrations

For any **nontrivial** w∈[F(b,c),F(b,c)], center Z(B⋊_{φ_w}Z) is trivial. A central element (g,t^n) must commute with b and c (fixed by φ_w), so g belongs to C_B(b)∩C_B(c)={1}, since noncommuting primitive free generators have intersecting centralizers {1}. Then t^n must commute with a, requiring φ_w^n(a)=a; but φ_w^n(a)=a w^n and F(b,c) torsionfree, so n=0. For w=1 the group is B×Z with center the infinite cyclic t factor. Hence M_w and M_1 are not weakly homotopy equivalent, even with all unmarked group isomorphisms allowed.

**General tail lemma.** If two Top^R diagrams are *strictly constant* X and Y from a common sufficiently large real threshold and there exists a finite ε-interleaving after arbitrary objectwise WE zigzags of the **entire** diagrams, then X and Y are isomorphic in Ho(Top). Proof: the strict HI gives an ordinary interleaving in Fun(R,HoTop) after applying localization; take both shift identities on a real u past both tails (and u+ε,u+2ε) where transition maps are identities. The two localized maps X→Y→X and Y→X→Y are identities, so are inverse isomorphisms. Whole-diagram zigzags do not alter localized pointwise types. Therefore, for w≠1, d_IHC(F_w,F_1)=d_HI(F_w,F_1)=∞; the same holds for min-coordinate R² by evaluating a cofinal diagonal. There can be **no finite positive sharp HI value** for this canonical undying-tail IA filtration. This is an ordinary terminal-type obstruction, NOT the requested nonclock theorem.

## T288. The original w=[b,c] is ALREADY visible to the integral cup ring

Consider cup map μ:Λ²H¹(M_w;Z)→H²(M_w;Z) with basis A,B,C,T dual to a,b,c,t, and U_a,U_b,U_c dual to the 2-cells. The standard finite presentation 2-complex Fox-diagonal formula (Fenn–Sjerve 1987, Theorem 2.3 and its finite-2-complex extension) evaluates μ from degree-two Magnus/Fox coefficients of relators. For w=1, μ(T∧A)=U_a, μ(T∧B)=U_b, μ(T∧C)=U_c (sign conventions harmless) and μ vanishes on Λ²⟨A,B,C⟩. For w=[b,c], its nonzero γ2/γ3 commutator coefficient contributes μ(B∧C)=±U_a, with all other components unchanged. Consequently ker μ_1=Λ²⟨A,B,C⟩, whose every rational 2-vector η satisfies η∧η=0 in Λ⁴H¹_Q. But ker μ_[b,c] contains η=T∧A∓B∧C, with η∧η=∓2 T∧A∧B∧C ≠0 in Λ⁴H¹_Q. The GL(H¹)-invariant property 'every kernel 2-vector has exterior square zero' differs. Thus even the *unmarked integral cohomology rings* M_1 and M_[b,c] are not isomorphic. Homology-blind in S109 does not mean cohomology-ring-blind.

## T289. Higher IA word repairs the ring blindness, but not HHA novelty

Let w_3=[[b,c],b]∈γ3F(b,c), a freely reduced nontrivial word. Since every w∈γ3 has *zero degree≤2 Magnus coefficients*, the degree-two coefficients of r_a(w) equal those of r_a(1); r_b and r_c are literally unchanged. The same argument applies to EVERY partial K_{w,j} (the a-relator is absent for j<4). By the presentation-CW cup formula, natural graded integral cohomology **ring diagrams** H*(F_{w_3};Z) and H*(F_1;Z) are isomorphic at all real stages, compatibly with restriction maps. In fact any nontrivial w∈γ3 F(b,c) has this property. Yet their π1 centers still differ and, by T287, whole-real unpointed HI is ∞. In particular ordinary persistence HOMOLOGY AND CUP RINGS together do not classify these filtered spaces. This is a careful elementary filtered packaging of classical lower-central/Fox machinery, not an independently new or quantitatively finite theorem.

At j0, j1 there are no 2-cells and ordinary exterior pair products map into H²=0; at j2 and j3 only common commutator relators appear, so naturality of ring isomorphism includes these stages. We do **not** assert that triple Massey products or twisted local systems match, nor that a particular degree-three Massey product is defined with zero indeterminacy when common degree-two commutator relations remain present.

## T290. Rescaling rules out a monodromy-only finite *numerical* obstruction

For λ>0, define the rescaled real diagram R_λF(u)=F(u/λ). Every ε-interleaving of F,G, with both shifted identities and arbitrary objectwise WE zigzags, induces a λε-interleaving of R_λF,R_λG by applying R_λ; conversely by 1/λ. Therefore d_HI(R_λF,R_λG)=λd_HI(F,G) for finite values and also ∞; likewise for d_IHC, including R² with uniform coordinate scaling. Free-group conjugacy class, the finite CW objects and their monodromy are unchanged by this reparameterization. Thus no theorem can supply a **positive finite scale-invariant universal numerical bound solely from an unweighted monodromy-class distinction**. An actual geometric metric/height/controlled-noise input is indispensable. This is a standard dimensional-covariance no-go, not a new HHA quantitative stability theorem; it does NOT rule out a naturally scaled geometric theorem.

## T291. Stability attempt and boundary/identity countercontrols

An exact filtration-preserving *natural homotopy equivalence* (e.g., actual cellular filtration-preserving conjugacy with inverse and filtered homotopies) trivially gives zero HI; under a geometrically controlled filtration shift, the usual sublevel-set interleaving is at most that control shift. Neither is independently novel nor establishes a new quantitative reconstruction theorem for nonconjugate IA monodromy. Countercontrols: w=1 gives identity/center Z and dHI0; inner automorphism φ=Inn(g) yields semidirect product isomorphic to B×Z after stable-letter replacement, so a raw nonidentity word is NOT an invariant; orientation/inverse word changes presentations but not the center test; w=[b,c] fails ring blindness (T288); w∈γ3 survives ring blindness but fails finite HI (T287). No claimed filtered stable-monodromy theorem evades all these controls in S110.
