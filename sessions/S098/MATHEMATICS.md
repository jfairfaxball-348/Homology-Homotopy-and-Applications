## T234: genuine global mixed class, cyclic restrictions, automorphisms, Bockstein

Let G=C2×C2=F2², A=F2 with trivial G-action. Use H*(BG;F2)=F2[x,y], |x|=|y|=1. Coordinate characters x(a,b)=a, y(a,b)=b define normalized 1-cocycles. Define the explicit inhomogeneous normalized 3-cocycle
$$
\kappa(g,h,l)=x(g)x(h)y(l)+x(g)y(h)y(l)\pmod2.
$$
It represents k=x²y+xy²=xy(x+y) and is closed since both summands are cup products of closed characters. Its two monomials are linearly independent in the polynomial cohomology ring, so k≠0.

The three nonzero proper cyclic subgroups of G are generated by (1,0),(0,1),(1,1). Substitute respectively (x,y)=(t,0),(0,t),(t,t) to obtain 0,0,t³+t³=0. These are all three restrictions. Every GL2(F2) coordinate change permutes the three nonzero linear forms x,y,x+y; their product k is fixed. In particular, k is NOT automorphism-conjugate to zero. Conversely a homogeneous cubic vanishing on the two axes has no x³,y³ monomials; vanishing on the diagonal then requires equal x²y and xy² coefficients. Exactly {0,k} vanish on all three subgroups. Finally, Sq¹(x)=x² and the Cartan formula give
$$
\boxed{k=Sq¹(xy).}
$$
This already makes the proposed distinction PRIMARY, not inherently higher/secondary.

## T235: explicit simplicial finite-rule model and finite 4-skeleton

Let B_•G be the group bar nerve: (B_•G)_p=G^p (exactly 4^p simplices before degeneracies). Let K(A,n)_p=Z^n(Δ^p;A) be the standard simplicial cochain Eilenberg–Mac Lane model. The standard cochain path object C²(Δ^•;A) is contractible and its differential δ:C²(Δ^•;A)→Z³(Δ^•;A) is a simplicial fibration with fiber Z²(Δ^•;A)=K(A,2). These standard simplicial path/fibration properties are OPENLY DECLARED classical inputs, not machine-verified results of the checker.

The explicit group cocycle κ induces a simplicial map B_•G→K(A,3). Form the simplicial pullback
$$
E_{\kappa,p}={(g_1,\ldots,g_p,b)\mid g_i\in G, b\in C²(Δ^p;A), \delta b=\kappa|_{(g_1,\ldots,g_p)}}.
$$
Faces/degeneracies are induced by simplex restriction/repetition and those of B_•G. Each p-level is FINITE (at most 4^p·2^{\binom{p+1}{3}} possibilities, an overcount). The realization |Eκ| is a CW model for the homotopy fiber of BG→K(A,3), hence an actual Postnikov 2-type with π1=G, π2=A, trivial action, first k-invariant κ and vanishing π_i for i>2.

Define Xκ=|Eκ|^{(4)}. It is an ACTUAL finite 4-dimensional CW complex because only finitely many nondegenerate simplices occur in degrees ≤4. Attaching cells of dimensions ≥5 cannot change π1,π2,π3, nor homology below dimension 4, so Xκ still has π1=G, π2=A, trivial G-action and the same first Postnikov class k (determined by the 2-type). **Xκ need not have vanishing π4 or any higher π. It is NOT the whole infinite Postnikov space.** This is a precise constructive finite rule, not an enumerated list of individual 4-simplices.

For κ=0, P0≃BG×K(A,2); Pk has globally nonzero k even after unmarked GL2 reidentification by T234.

## T236: the finite complexes have DIFFERENT integral H2

For K(F2,2)→Pκ→BG, use the mod-two Serre cohomology spectral sequence. H¹(K(F2,2);F2)=0 and H²(K(F2,2);F2)=F2·u. The defining transgression is d3(u)=κ∈H³(BG;F2). H²(BG;F2)=span{x²,xy,y²} has dimension three and survives in degree two. Since there are no other total-degree-two terms or differentials,
$$
\dim H²(P_0;F2)=4,\q\quad\\dim H²(P_k;F2)=3.
$$
The 4-skeleta have the same H² as the corresponding full Postnikov spaces.

For **integral homology**, H1(Pκ;Z)=G=(Z/2)², H2(BG;Z)=Z/2 by Künneth, and H2(K(F2,2);Z)=Z/2. The only possible incoming homology Serre differential into E_{0,2} is d3:H3(BG;Z)→Z/2. It is zero both for κ=0 and κ=Sq¹(xy). In the latter case it is evaluation of κ on reductions of integral H3 cycles; for every integral cycle z the homology mod-2 Bockstein of its reduction vanishes because it lifts as a Z/4 cycle. The Bockstein adjunction then gives <Sq¹(xy),red z>=<xy,β_hom(red z)>=0. Thus in both cases the integral Serre filtration yields
$$
0\to Z/2\to H2(P_\kappa;Z)\to Z/2\to0.
$$
The group H2 has order four, but the extension may or may not split.

Now mod-2 UCT in cohomology gives
$$
0\to Ext(H1(P_\kappa;Z),F2)\cong F2²\to H²(P_\kappa;F2)\to Hom(H2(P_\kappa;Z),F2)\to0.
$$
The dimensions of the rightmost Hom are 2 for κ=0 and 1 for κ=k. The only order-four abelian groups with these Hom ranks are respectively (Z/2)² and Z/4. Therefore for actual finite CW 4-skeleta:
$$
\boxed{H_2(X_0;\mathbb Z)\cong (\mathbb Z/2)^2,\quad H_2(X_k;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z/4.}
$$
The conclusion uses a non-split extension, which was NOT implied merely by the vanishing integral d3; the UCT computation is essential. Arbitrary ordinary unpointed weak equivalences preserve these homology groups. Thus any whole-real pair of filtrations containing these two terminal stages at the same time cannot have isomorphic *graded integral persistent homology*. This kills the specifically proposed equal-homology comparison, not every imaginable finite Postnikov pair.

## T237: cyclic-local splitting, global primary detection

Since each restriction of k to BC2 vanishes, the pullback **2-type** over every nontrivial cyclic subgroup is (noncanonically) equivalent to BC2×K(F2,2). This does not furnish compatible simultaneous trivializations or a canonical finite CW filtration isomorphism.

The Postnikov projection pκ:Pκ→BG has pκ*k=0 when κ=k, since κ is killed by its defining transgression/nullhomotopy. Hence Sq¹(p_k*(xy))=0. For the product P0, projection p0 induces an injection on H³(BG;F2), so Sq¹(p_0*(xy))=p0*k≠0. This difference is PRIMARY Steenrod data, and ordinary integral H2 already distinguishes the spaces. It is therefore false to describe the k versus zero pair as a secondary, integral-homology-invisible persistence example.
