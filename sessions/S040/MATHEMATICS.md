## Setup and correct derived-[2] model (T91)
Take five nonempty connected simply connected finite CW spaces with ordinary maps
`A --f--> B --g--> C --h--> D --k--> E`. The real filtration is empty before 0, A,B,C,D,E on consecutive half-open unit intervals [0,1),...,[4,5), and point after 5; G is empty before 0 and point thereafter. A positive strict/interleaving distance assertion requires **both** identities on the whole real indexing poset and *arbitrary* objectwise weak-equivalent replacements, not a failed point-set representative.

The two-shift map restricted to integer stages 0,1,2 is the strictly natural morphism
`theta2: (A --f--> B --g--> C) -> (C --h--> D --k--> E)`
whose components are (gf,hg,kh). A homotopy-category assertion `theta2=point` is in Ho(Top^[2]), not three equations in HoTop. To compute its mapping class use a **projectively cofibrant** QX of X=(A->B->C): take A_c=A, replace B by the mapping cylinder of f (so A_c->B_c is a cofibration), then replace C by the mapping cylinder of the induced B_c->C (so B_c->C_c is a cofibration). There are natural stagewise homotopy equivalences QX->X, and QX is projectively cofibrant for [2] (using the latching/cofibration criterion for linear direct categories). For fibrant target Y=(C->D->E) the simplicial/topological diagram mapping space is the strict equalizer

`Map_[2](QX,Y) = {(u,v,w) in Map(A_c,C) x Map(B_c,D) x Map(C_c,E) | v f_c = h u, w g_c = k v}`.

Use singular/simplicial mapping spaces if point-set function-space hypotheses are awkward. Its path-components compute `Hom_Ho(Top^[2])(X,Y)`. The actual theta2 is the triple composed with QX->X. Exact **zero condition**: its vertex in this equalizer is connected by a path of strict natural transformations to a constant natural transformation `(c,h(c),kh(c))` with image factoring through the point diagram (more generally a point-diagram map with compatible choices). Equivalently, its coherent component is zero. This path includes BOTH naturality equations at every time, with higher compatibility encoded by QX. Three separately chosen nullhomotopies are not enough. A homotopy-coherent description on the unreplaced X includes component nullhomotopies, two square homotopies, and compatibility for their overlap; it is not licensed to ignore the final coherence.

At exactly epsilon=1 the two-shift structure maps of F restricted to [0,3) are precisely theta2 (with repeated constant-stage copies on half-open intervals and terminal point thereafter). The condition theta2=point is the necessary obstruction from an IHC interleaving; an extension to full real-diagram IHC factorization can be assembled over these interval-constant pieces, but **this does not yet strictify the primitive f,g,h,k**. The unresolved relative lifting problem is whether a null component here belongs to the image, on weak-equivalence classes, of the functor sending a chain with all three length-two composites strictly constant to theta2. Need to compare the homotopy fibre of this lifting map, not merely pi0 vertex maps.

## T92 — proved restricted five-stage strictification when ANY primitive arrow is null
**Statement.** Assume theta2=point in Ho(Top^[2]). If at least one of f,g,h,k is nullhomotopic, there exists a full five-stage diagram equivalent by zigzags of objectwise weak equivalences to the original, with gf,hg,kh all *literally constant*, with compatible propagated values. Hence the point-step comparison has a strict real epsilon=1 interleaving; at this threshold **no strict dHI>dIHC witness** arises. The finite CW claim is on homotopy types of stages, not path-object intermediary spaces. This is an internal consequence of T87 and standard mapping-cylinder HEP; not asserted literature-new.

Proof, cases (ordinary unpointed maps and connected target point choices):
1. `f~constant`: replace f by a constant via the full-chain path-space span of T87. Restrict theta2 to the last two source/target nodes B->C and D->E: the derived-arrow morphism (hg,kh) is point-null. Apply S039-T87 to the four-stage suffix B->C->D->E. T87's construction **does not replace its first object B**; its changing outgoing maps do not affect the already constant A->B. Thus gf remains constant while hg and kh become constant.
2. `k~constant`: similarly make k constant; restriction of theta2 to first two nodes is the point-null derived arrow (gf,hg). Apply T87 to A->B->C->D. Reattach E with a constant D'->E, transporting the chosen constant through the natural homotopy-equivalence zigzag. All three pairs are now constant.
3. `h~constant`: use an edge-homotopy span to make h literally constant. theta2 point-null forces gf:A->C null in HoTop. Mapping-cylinder-factor f as a cofibration, use HEP to deform g so gf is literally constant. Because h is already constant, hg and kh remain literally constant.
4. `g~constant`: make g literally constant, so gf and hg are literally constant. theta2 point-null forces kh:C->E null in HoTop. Replace h:C->D by the cylinder cofibration C↪M_h, transport k through M_h->D, and use HEP on the nullhomotopy kh to deform transported k with k'|C constant. Then kh becomes constant and g remains constant. All individual arrow homotopies and cylinder replacements are made as **full-chain** weak-equivalence spans, not presumed equalities of diagram maps.

In each case the three point images agree on the shared domain by actual functorial composition. Choosing one point in A and propagating through f',g',h',k' provides a strictly natural section G->F'^1, and the unique F'->G^1 maps give both strict interleaving identities at every real t. For t in [0,3), shift 2 maps vanish by the three literal length-two compositions; later targets are point; earlier sources are empty. The other identity is automatic on point-stalks. **Corollary (necessary condition for a five-stage epsilon=1 separation):** all four primitive arrow homotopy classes must be nonzero. This is a pruning theorem, not sufficiency for an obstruction and not general dHI/dIHC equality.

## T93 — five-stage threshold bookkeeping and a robust single-window control
All relevant half-step thresholds for the point target are 0, 1/2, 1, 3/2, 2, 5/2 (check t=0,...,4; at exact cut endpoints half-open strata follow the next object). At 0 the nonterminal stages must be contractible; at 1/2 each primitive arrow must be null; at 1 the entire derived theta2 is tested; at 3/2 the derived arrow `(A->B)->(D->E)` with components (hgf,khg) is tested, **not** just two vertexwise triples; at 2 the only surviving nonterminal shifted map is khgf:A->E; at 5/2 all shifted nonempty targets are point. For times strictly between consecutive cuts, conditions cannot weaken beyond those at the previous threshold, because the same integer-time restriction applies and shift monotonicity gives upper witnesses.

At epsilon=2, if khgf is null, factor the entire A->D composite as a cofibration by iterated mapping cylinders, transport E/k, then apply HEP to make the 4-fold composite strictly constant. Hence an explicit strict epsilon=2 interleaving exists after objectwise weak replacements. At epsilon=5/2 every filtration of this form interleaves with the point step. The genuinely unresolved *point-target* separation mechanisms are epsilon=1 (theta2 triple-coupling) and epsilon=3/2 (derived-arrow coupling for overlapping length-three composites); neither is settled merely by T87.

## T94 — exact explicit control with FOUR essential primitive maps but NO gap
Let W=S^3∨S^3, p2:W->S^3 the pinch projection onto the second summand, and i1:S^3->W the inclusion of the first. Set n=i1 p2. It induces a nonzero matrix on pi3(W)=Z², `[[0,1],[0,0]]`, so n is NOT nullhomotopic. But p2 i1 is literally the constant based map, hence n² is *literally* constant. Put A=B=C=D=E=W and f=g=h=k=n. Then all four primitive arrows are essential, while gf=hg=kh=n² are already strictly constant. Thus theta2 is point-null, and the point-step filtration has **dIHC=dHI=1**: 1 is achieved by the propagated-point section; no epsilon<1 works because (for 1/2<=epsilon<1) nonnull primitive arrows survive at integer time and (for epsilon<1/2) noncontractible stages survive. This construction explicitly refutes the tempting converse that 'all four arrows essential' itself supplies a higher obstruction. Elementary control, not an HHA-original theorem.

## Unresolved exact bridge
A five-stage counterexample to HI=IHC must provide (i) theta2=point in the *derived* [2]-mapping space, (ii) proof that NO objectwise equivalent five-chain has all gf,hg,kh strict constant, (iii) all four primitive maps essential by T92, (iv) full real naturality/both identities and a positive epsilon interval. A candidate at 3/2 needs the analogous derived-[1] triple composites and failure of relative strictification under all replacements. A failed search for a nullhomotopy or an isolated representative is NEVER a metric lower bound. No such witness is known from S040. Conversely no general five-stage equality proof was established.
