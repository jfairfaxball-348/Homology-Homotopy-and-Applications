## T39 — precise finite-to-real relative block dimension equality

Fix a field k, n>=2, finite nonempty chains G_i={a_i,0<...<a_i,m_i} embedded in R with **distinguished pre-birth sentinel** a_i,0 strictly below every genuine birth/critical value. Write G=∏G_i, P=R^n. Set π_i:R→G_i by clamping everything below a_i,0 to a_i,0 and otherwise taking the largest grid value <=t (for t beyond the last grid point, clamp to the last). Thus π:P→G is coordinatewise monotone and π|G=id. Define E=π*; it repeats every grid stalk on its half-open stratum, including the two unbounded tails.

Let X_G and X_P be additive closures of **ALL** LLO n-block indicator modules respectively, using the original LLO 2D definition (birth quadrants, death quadrants, one-axis bands) and its recursive nD definition (birth/death products and full-axis-induced blocks). E_X is the ordinary pointwise exact structure restricted to short sequences exact under Hom(B,-) for **every** such block B. No ordinary-projective substitution.

**T39:** For every finite-dimensional G-diagram V and every integer k>=0,
    pd_(X_P)(E V) <= k  iff  pd_(X_G)(V) <= k.
In particular, for one-critical finite simplicial R^n multifiltrations whose grades lie strictly above each sentinel, the real-parameter degree-j homology diagram is E of its sampled G-diagram: the finite relative block resolution length k equals its original real-parameter relative block resolution length k. For fixed n,k the T37 finite-grid polynomial arithmetic procedure therefore decides this **specific block class** of the original LLO Problem 1 for explicitly one-critical simplicial inputs. It does not settle arbitrary fixed interval families, arbitrary finitely presented modules/presentations, n or k as input, or bit/runtime optimisation.

### Proof, with the easily missed left-adjoint test

(1) π* is exact pointwise and restriction R_G along G⊂P satisfies R_G E=id. Each G block becomes an actual P block under E: a finite-chain upper ray becomes a real upper ray at its first included threshold, a finite-chain initial lower ray becomes a real lower ray with its last included threshold extended to the next critical value (or all R if it reaches the final grid element), and a one-axis interval becomes a real interval; full-axis factors stay full because π clamps the left tail to the sentinel. Products/recursive full-axis inductions preserve this description. This argument FAILS for the alternative zero-extension below the earliest grid point: its finite death blocks acquire artificial lower birth faces.

(2) For EVERY real block B, consider Lan_π B. The usual left Kan formula evaluates at g∈G as colim over π^{-1}(G_{<=g}). This indexing subposet is a product of initial real rays (possibly all R) and is filtered. For a 1D interval indicator on R, its colimit over such a ray is either k or 0. As the cutoff g increases, the nonzero values form a consecutive finite-chain interval. An upper real ray yields an upper interval (possibly full or empty), and a lower real ray yields a lower interval (possibly full or empty): after any later zero appears, it kills earlier elements in the colimit. Finite tensor products commute with these filtered colimits over k; hence Lan_π sends an n-block (the prescribed product of upper rays, lower rays, or an induced full-axis block) to a G-block or zero. A factor becoming full simply reduces active-axis count and is covered by the LLO recursive induced/full-band clause.

(3) The adjunction Lan_π ⊣ π* yields NATURAL isomorphisms
 Hom_P(B,E W) = Hom_G(Lan_π B,W)
for each P block B and any W over G. Thus E carries ANY G E_X-admissible short exact sequence to a P E_X-admissible short exact sequence. Together with (1), this shows pd_P(E V)<=pd_G(V). Note: pointwise exactness alone would NOT justify this step.

(4) For k=0, conversely if E V is P block-projective, restrict its block decomposition to G; each real block restricts to a finite G block or zero (upper/lower/full-axis support preserves its type), so V=R_G EV is in X_G. Thus pd_P(E V)=0 iff pd_G(V)=0. Even when one uses add(X) as idempotent closure, finite-grid Krull–Schmidt plus restriction preserves the conclusion.

(5) For k>=1 choose an admissible G X-projective epimorphism Q→V with kernel K (T37 provides one). Its E-image is an admissible P X-projective epimorphism E Q→E V, with kernel E K by exactness. Standard relative dimension shifting in an exact category with enough chosen projectives gives, for either index category, pd(V)<=k iff pd(K)<=k-1 for ANY such admissible projective epimorphism (relative Schanuel / comparison lemma, including pd0). Induct using k=0 and the same E-induced epimorphism: pd_P(E V)<=k iff pd_P(E K)<=k-1 iff pd_G(K)<=k-1 iff pd_G(V)<=k. No claim that arbitrary P resolutions stay constructible or that restriction preserves arbitrary relative E_X sequences is needed.

(6) Geometry: If a finite one-critical simplicial filtration has a finite set of birth coordinates, choose one real sentinel s_i below all births on each axis. For t∈R^n, membership of any simplex with birth g is equivalent to g<=π(t), where π uses all birth thresholds and sentinel; hence the CHAIN complexes and inclusion chain maps at t and π(t) are literally identical. Homology commutes with these identical diagrams. Fixed n has at most (N+1)^n grid stalks and T37's bound applies to this specified expanded representation.

### T39 failure mode if the sentinel is omitted — rigorous counterexample

Take the naive G0={0,1}² and U=k at (0,0), zero at the other three grades. This is a G0 **death block**, so pd_X,G0(U)=0. Extend to P=R² by zero below the first grid point, constant in strata: the module W=k_[0,1)×[0,1). Its restriction to G0 is U. But W is NOT P-block-projective. The LLO 2-middle square at x=-1<0.5 and y=0.5<1.5 has stalks (bottom,left,right,top)=(0,0,k,0), giving Koszul 0→0→k→0 and nonzero H_1=k. Thus LLO Theorem 4/14 rules out real block decomposition. Independently, End(W)=k for the connected rectangle interval, so W is indecomposable but its support is neither an upper quadrant, lower quadrant nor an infinite horizontal/vertical band.

This is not an exotic module: start with a triangulated circle at birth (0,0), attach two distinct simplicial disks along its entire common boundary at grades (1,0) and (0,1) respectively, and at the join let both disks be present. All faces carry grade <= cofaces after compatible subdivision. Its H_1 persistence is exactly W, while H_2 at grades >=(1,1) may additionally be nonzero. The naive 2×2 grid projectivity test misses a mixed birth/death corner; the sentinel-augmented 3×3 grid exposes it. This witness disproves **unaugmented-grid equivalence**, not T39.

### Adversarial k/dimension cases

Zero module: both dimensions zero. Full-axis blocks: clamping MUST preserve the entire left infinite tail. A death block at minimum grid index on an unaugmented zero-extension becomes a bounded rectangle (the counterexample). With a sentinel and actual one-critical input, each sentinel-coordinate hyperface has zero simplices, so clamping agrees with the true original filtration. Exactness of π* is pointwise; **only** the Lan_π block computation upgrades it to relative E_X exactness. The finite-grid polynomial statement holds for fixed n and k and an actual constructive one-critical filtration, not arbitrary free presentations. For n=2 the proof uses birth/death quadrants and horizontal/vertical bands; n>=3 uses the ORIGINAL recursively induced definition, not arbitrary box intervals.

## T40 — a TRUE radius-plus-one-height Euclidean bifiltration with nonzero 2D homotopy defect

For a finite Euclidean point set V with one scalar height h, set X_(r,t)=VR_r({v:h(v)<=t}), with all simplex and filtration maps literal inclusions. For the four corners A=X_(r0,t0), B=X_(r1,t0), C=X_(r0,t1), D=X_(r1,t1), the latching homotopy colimit over the punctured square is homotopy equivalent to U=B∪_A C, since A=B∩C as literal simplicial subcomplexes and the inclusions are cofibrations. The canonical comparison is U↪D; its mapping cofiber is D/U (homotopy cofiber). This is a genuine 2-parameter invariant, not just objectwise persistence homology.

**Three points, minimal H2 defect.** Coordinates p=(0,0), q=(2,0) with height0; z=(1,0) with height1; choose r0=1, r1=2, t0=0, t1=1. Distances d(p,z)=d(q,z)=1 and d(p,q)=2, all exact. A={p,q} with no edge (f-vector vertices2/edges0); B={p,q,pq} (2/1); C=path p-z-q (3/2); D=filled triangle (3/3/one 2-simplex). U is exactly the triangle boundary, hence S1; D is the closed 2-simplex and contractible, so the CANONICAL mixed cofiber is S2 and H2=Z. At every individual grade among these four, H2=0; the mixed 2D comparison detects a different feature. This is SHARP for an H2 mixed defect: with at most two vertices every D is at most one-dimensional, and the simplicial inclusion U⊂D has H2(D,U)=0 because C2(D,U)=0.

**Four points, rank-two defect.** Let a=(0,0), b=(1,0), c=(1,1), d=(0,1), heights h(a)=h(c)=0, h(b)=h(d)=1, r0=1 and r1=√2. Four sides have length1, two diagonals √2. A: two isolated opposite lows (2/0); B: their diagonal segment (2/1); C: the unfilled square C4 (4/4); D: the full tetrahedron Δ3 (4/6/4/1 in dimensions0/1/2/3). U=C4 plus its a-c diagonal edge, a connected graph with 4 vertices, 5 edges, so β1=5−4+1=2. No triangles are present in U because its simplices come from B OR C, not from the flag completion of their union. Thus U≃S1∨S1 and the mixed cofiber D/U≃ΣU≃S2∨S2, with integral H2=Z². In all four individual spaces H2=0. This uses the SAME fixed intrinsic Euclidean distance matrix and precisely ONE height parameter.

**Quantitative perturbation:** In either example let L be the next distance after the side length1 (L=2 for the collinear triple; L=√2 for square). Independently displace each point by Euclidean norm <=ε and change each height by <=δ, with 4ε<L−1 and 2δ<1. Use adapted cutoffs r0'=1+2ε, r1'=L+2ε, t0'=δ,t1'=1+δ. Triangle inequality shows all formerly short edges still have length <=r0', all formerly long edges have length >=L−2ε>r0', and all pairs have distance <=r1'. Heights keep the low/high partition. All four abstract simplicial complexes and their literal inclusions therefore remain combinatorially identical. Consequently the mixed cofibers and H2 ranks are constant throughout this **explicit perturbation window**. This is stability of this finite combinatorial witness with adjusted cutoffs, NOT a general homotopical interleaving theorem.

**Verification scope:** A separate bounded deterministic Python check evaluated exactly the specified 3- and 4-point configurations, enumerated subsets using Euclidean pairwise distances and counted simplex/edge/connected-component ranks. It returned triple f-vectors A(2,0,0), B(2,1,0), C(3,2,0), D(3,3,1), U β1=1; square A(2,0,0,0), B(2,1,0,0), C(4,4,0,0), D(4,6,4,1), U β1=2. This finite check is supplementary only: the exact distances, union graph Euler ranks, cofibration and cofiber proofs above are independent symbolic arguments. NO exhaustive search or computed novelty certificate.

## Front C mixed integer grids — no unsupported promotion

On [0,2]^2 and [0,2]^3, the S015 obstruction remains: fieldwise decompositions over Q and every Fp, primitive individual edge Smith cokernels, and local middle bands do NOT supply a coherent family of integral global commuting idempotents. Attempting independent primitive-basis choices on each square provides no cross-square compatibility equations; one cannot infer a natural global splitting. We did not find a new numerical module satisfying **all** hypotheses that fails to split, and did not prove an all-grid iff. In particular, one induced middle band splits and is NOT a counterexample. Front C stays OPEN.

## Novelty and usefulness

T39 is a precise real-parameter bridge for explicitly one-critical, prebirth-augmented block filtrations and a specific block family, making S015's limitation technically sharper; it may address a restricted input/parameter subcase of LLO Problem1, but the construction depends heavily on published Kan extension, block theory, finite approximation and relative Schanuel. T40 is genuine Euclidean bifiltration geometry with literal finite witnesses and robust mixed cofibers; its mechanism (cofibrant union, graph cycles, suspension) is elementary and likely familiar to multi-filtration specialists. Neither is independently cleared as an HHA-sized ORIGINAL flagship; no Stage2 pass.
