1. Madahar–Sarkaria, original 2000 paper *A Minimal Triangulation of the Hopf Map and its Application* https://www.kssarkaria.org/docs/Hopf.pdf (SRC-0054 original author PDF proof-inspected in earlier session; during S025 re-found original linked document/search excerpts). Classical Hopf f1 and CP² cup square not newly found here.
2. Munson–Volić, *Cubical Homotopy Theory* (Cambridge 2015), Ch3 §§3.8–3.9 (SRC-0056/SRC-0060): previous indexed author-hosted original PDF gave total cubical cofiber; 45MB original could not be retrieved in S024; NO full direct PDF proof inspection this session. Square-derived cofiber NOT a new operation.
3. Mémoli–Stefanou–Zhou, JACT2024 published https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41468-023-00138-5 (SRC-0055), full original open HTML visible with Def3.3 on Blumberg–Lesnick homotopy-interleaving distance, stability of persistent cup invariants. S025 read original HTML excerpt and abstract, not each 2024 proof.
4. Blumberg–Lesnick 2017 author original https://www.math.columbia.edu/~thaddeus/blumberg/11.pdf (NEW SRC-0061, original indexed section8 and 2017 main theorem excerpts). Homotopy interleaving distance and persistent homotopy groups are preexisting concepts. Their index is 1-parameter; our R² analogue must not be falsely attributed as a verbatim sourced 2017 theorem.
5. Lanari–Scoccola, *Rectification of interleavings and a persistent Whitehead theorem*, AGT23(2023) 803–832 DOI10.2140/agt.2023.23.803 https://msp.org/agt/2023/23-2/agt-v23-n2-p05-p.pdf (NEW SRC-0062). Original published abstract and §5 excerpts explicitly address interleavings and persistent homotopy groups, with multiparameter degree-Rips example. We did NOT fully proof-inspect each theorem. Strong novelty risk: neither a vague 'persistent homotopy incomplete' slogan nor classical cofiber stability itself is new.
6. Oudot–Waas SoCG2026 original DOI10.4230/LIPIcs.SoCG.2026.82 (SRC-0049), and Blaser et al JACT2026 DOI10.1007/s41468-026-00237-z original §6.3 (SRC-0053), remain strong geometric-Rips/homotopy stability prior art from previous source audits; not reread fully in this session.
7. Maehara 1986 *Sphericity exceeds cubicity for almost all complete bipartite graphs*, original publisher abstract https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/009589568690081X (NEW SRC-0063) states sph K(n,n)≥n, possible dimension-specific obstruction beyond stars; no proved matching K_6,6 subgraph and proof not inspected. Not used for a new R5 exclusion.

**Priority decision:** T57 is an internally proved explicit numerical separation, not an original primitive of homotopy theory. A narrowly formulated fully Euclidean R181, ring-and-π-indistinguishability yet positive 2D dHI example is a possible incremental research note, but NOT proven absent from published precedents and NOT convincingly a noteworthy HHA flagship. No Stage2 novelty pass; a properly adversarial theorem-by-theorem equivalence search is S026 work, not implicitly completed here.
