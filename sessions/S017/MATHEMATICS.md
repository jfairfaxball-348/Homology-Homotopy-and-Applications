## T41 — all LLO blocks collapse to ordinary free projectives on a zero-sentinel boundary

Fix any field F, any integer n>=2, and finite chains G_i={0,1,...,m_i} with m_i>=1. Set G=∏G_i. Let X_G be the additive idempotent closure of exactly the LLO block indicators: birth upper quadrants, death lower quadrants, and bands induced along full coordinate axes (recursively in dimension n). Let C_G consist of finite-dimensional diagrams V on G with V(g)=0 whenever ANY coordinate g_i=0. Let pd_X use the pointwise exact structure whose conflations remain exact under Hom_G(B,-) for every block B; let pd_fr be ordinary projective dimension relative to sums of representables F_{G>=p}. For every V∈C_G and every integer k>=0,
    pd_X(V)<=k  iff  pd_fr(V)<=k.
Both dimensions are at most n; all fields, including char2, and arbitrary finite grid sizes.

**Lemma 1 (no nonbirth tests).** Every non-birth LLO block B has a lower-ray factor or a full-axis factor in some coordinate i. For every g in supp(B), replace g_i by 0 to obtain q≤g still in supp(B), with B(q→g)=id and V(q)=0. Naturality of every f:B→V gives f_g=V(q→g)f_q=0. Thus Hom_G(B,V)=0. Birth blocks whose support avoids all coordinate-zero hyperfaces are precisely principal upsets G_{>=p} with every p_i>=1. All other birth blocks also have a full coordinate factor and satisfy the same vanishing lemma. This accounts for the n-dimensional recursively induced cases, not only 2D bands.

**Lemma 2 (a genuinely admissible free cover).** Take the standard ordinary free epimorphism ε:P=⊕_{p: p_i>=1}F_{>=p}⊗_F V(p)→V, at grade g sending a generator from p≤g by V(p≤g). It is pointwise onto because the p=g summand maps identically onto V(g); P vanishes on every sentinel hyperface, and so does K=ker ε. To prove E_X-admissibility, for every non-birth block Hom(B,V)=0; every remaining birth block is a representable F_{>=p}, and Hom(F_{>=p},-) is exact for pointwise epis. Therefore Hom(B,P)→Hom(B,V) surjects for EVERY LLO block, and 0→K→P→V→0 is an admissible X-projective cover. A mere pointwise epi would not be enough without this Hom argument.

**Base k=0.** If V is X-projective, by finite Krull–Schmidt it is a direct summand of a finite sum of indecomposable LLO blocks. Every summand has zero stalk on the sentinel hyperfaces since dimensions are nonnegative there. Any nonbirth block has nonzero support on such a hyperface and therefore cannot occur; the remaining blocks are representables and their summands are ordinary projective. Conversely every ordinary projective on the zero-face subcategory is a summand of birth representables. Thus pd_X(V)=0 iff pd_fr(V)=0, including V=0.

**Induction.** For k>=1 apply relative Schanuel/dimension-shifting to the admissible free ε above, and ordinary dimension shifting to that same ε. They give pd_X(V)<=k iff pd_X(K)<=k−1 and pd_fr(V)<=k iff pd_fr(K)<=k−1. K∈C_G, so the inductive hypothesis applies. Finite product-of-chains ordinary free resolutions have length <=n by tensoring the coordinatewise length-one bar/Koszul resolutions, hence dimensions numerically agree. No characteristic-dependent cancellation is used; alternating signs satisfy d²=0 also in characteristic2 by paired cancellation. For n=2,3,4 the same coordinate-wise argument applies without altered hypotheses.

**Adversarial 3×3 test.** On G={0,1,2}² let V be F only at (1,1) and zero elsewhere. It satisfies full sentinel-hyperface vanishing. The ordinary minimal free resolution is
0→F_{>=(2,2)}→F_{>=(2,1)}⊕F_{>=(1,2)}→F_{>=(1,1)}→V→0,
with alternating incidence signs. The top 2D corner Koszul H₂ is nonzero, so pd_fr=pd_X=2. On the UNSENTINELLED {1,2}² subgrid, this restriction is a death block of relative dimension 0. This simultaneously tests the characteristic2 signs and the necessity of whole zero coordinate faces, not just a zero minimal vertex.

## T41b — finite free-presented R^n version and originality killer

Let M:R^n→Vect_F be given by a finite multigraded presentation between finite sums of principal upset modules, with finitely many homogeneous generator and relation vectors. On each axis choose a real sentinel s_i strictly below EVERY generator and relation grade coordinate. Let G_i comprise s_i and all those coordinates, and π:R^n→G be the S016 left-tail clamping/floor map. At every real grade t, the generator and relation free stalks and homogeneous presentation maps are exactly the corresponding stalks at π(t), since c≤t iff c≤π(t) for each presentation grade c. Consequently M≅π*(M|_G), and V=M|_G vanishes on EVERY sentinel-coordinate hyperface. T39's independently reviewed relative equivalence gives pd_all-LLO-real(M)=pd_X,G(V); T41 makes this pd_fr,G(V), the ordinary multigraded free projective dimension. Restrict/extend the standard free resolutions to identify it with ordinary pd_fr,R(M). For fixed n and fixed k the sampled representation has at most (q+1)^n grid stalks for q total grades and permits classical polynomial field-operation algorithms; this is NOT a bit-complexity statement uniform in n,k, nor an algorithm for a different fixed interval family. It refutes the notion that the all-block fp subcase alone constitutes a novel solution of the published general Problem1, and does not contradict T39 for arbitrary V not vanishing on sentinel faces.

**T39 hostile review.** For each g except terminal, π^{-1}(G_{<=g}) is a real INITIAL ray ending just before the next threshold; at maximal g it is ALL R. The left Kan colimit of any real interval indicator over the initial ray is F on a consecutive set of grid grades, zero otherwise: upper real ray maps to upper grid interval, lower real ray to lower grid interval, full axis to full; any interval maps to interval or zero. Death intervals die once the initial ray includes a zero *after* their endpoint; this includes terminal unbounded fibre. Tensor products commute with these filtered vector-space colimits, so the actual LLO n-block product/recursive full-axis types map to blocks/zero. By adjunction Hom_R(B,π*W)=Hom_G(Lan_πB,W), π* carries G E_X-conflations to real E_X-conflations. Restricting real blocks gives G blocks/zero and the k=0 converse; relative Schanuel on a chosen admissible G cover inductively proves both inequalities, all k and characteristics. This proof requires genuine lower sentinel clamping; it is FALSE for arbitrary zero-extension of an unsentinelled finite grid (S016 bounded-rectangle counterexample). No assertion of a new Kan construction: original LLO §2.2 and Botnan–Oppermann–Oudot 2024/25 supply direct published antecedents.

## T42 — general one-height Euclidean Rips cup no-go at maximal radius

Let P be a finite nonempty metric point cloud (Euclidean special case) with ONE height h and cutoffs r0≤r1, t0≤t1. Write A=VR_r0(P_{h<=t0}), B=VR_r1(P_{h<=t0}), C=VR_r0(P_{h<=t1}), D=VR_r1(P_{h<=t1}). The strict subcomplexes satisfy A=B∩C, so the punctured-square homotopy colimit is U=B∪C, and its canonical comparison is inclusion U↪D. If r1≥diam(P_{h<=t1}), D is a full simplex, hence contractible. For nonempty U, the homotopy cofiber D/U is equivalent to the reduced suspension ΣU; suspensions have nullhomotopic reduced diagonal and zero reduced cup products over ANY coefficient ring, including every cup square. BOTH S016 collinear-triple and square examples use r1 equal to this diameter, and thus cannot realize a nonzero mixed-cofiber Hopf cup square. This DOES NOT imply vanishing homotopy groups or stable operations; and no obstruction is asserted when r1<diam and D is noncontractible. Elementary classical topology, not journal-original.
