## T221 — full equivariant modular chain equality does NOT distinguish lens actions

Since B is local with maximal ideal (t), each u_q is a UNIT (construct the inverse by a finite geometric series). ## T222 — exact two-lane ALL-REAL sharp metric discrepancy

This is stronger than isomorphic graded homology: complete Cp group-ring chain diagrams are equal after legitimate whole-diagram QI roofs.

## T223 — a genuinely multistage CW skeletal extension, quantitative lower bound

## T224 — orbitwise stability of the top-versus-chain discrepancy
Take either literal final finite Q-CW cone K, with fixed Q-invariant face-monotone birth times λ_e per Q-cell orbit producing any filtration above; take independent admissible face-monotone μ_e with sup_e|μ_e−λ_e|≤h on the SAME K (fixed action/attaching maps). Then inclusions K_s^λ⊂K_(s+h)^μ and vice versa provide strict equivariant h-interleavings satisfying both identities; functorial B-cellular chains yield h-interleavings too. For the TWO original lens filtrations, if each side is independently so perturbed by at most h, triangle inequality yields
 dHI^QTop(F',G') ≥ T/2−2h,     dHI^Ch(B)(C_*F',C_*G') ≤ 2h.
Hence the discrepancy is ≥T/2−4h>0 whenever h<T/8. For four-active-block skeletal filtration use lower (d−c)/2 and obtain discrepancy ≥(d−c)/2−4h>0 for h<(d−c)/8. This is standard fixed-CW cellweight sublevel stability coupled with the new application, NOT robustness under changing deck group/action/attaching maps or general GH perturbations.
