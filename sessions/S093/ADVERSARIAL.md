# S093 ADVERSARIAL — serious falsifiers and exact limitations
1. **Integral arithmetic:** cyclic differentials are alternating (g−1), N_Z, NOT t,t over ZQ. Torus d₂=(1−y,x−1) is not the mod2 same-sign vector. All d²=0 checked before reducing to k.
2. **Topological action:** the Q deck sums are genuine continuous maps only after reduced suspension pinch/fold; Q abelian ensures deck maps equivariant. Collapsed orbit creates Q-fixed BASEPOINT; NOT a globally free Q-CW complex. Any demand for an entirely free *based* target is incompatible with the collapsed basepoint here.
3. **False universal minimality:** n=2 null and n=3 nonnull concern THIS norm map on THIS periodic cellular family. This is neither a theorem about arbitrary perfect complexes nor all finite Q-CW maps. Even n have explicit nullhomotopies; n=1 is not a ghost.
4. **Noncyclic false homotopy:** torus h(e₁)=u+αuv, h(e₂)=v+βuv makes h d₂=0, although needed N=uv; no hidden B-linear homotopy or chain-null class. Homology dimensions (H₂,H₁)=(1,5) and N acts zero.
5. **Equivariant null:** a based Q-nullhomotopy would induce a B-cellular chain nullhomotopy. Contradiction rules out equivariant nullity, not nonequivariant topological nullity. Forgetful underlying k-chain nullity is weaker still.
6. **Whole diagrams:** cone final stalk is contractible rather than chain-zero. Need natural whole-diagram comparison/roof and point contraction for Top. All real indices and endpoints; no unsupported inference from vertexwise null maps.
7. **HI vs IHC:** equvariant Ch(B) has HI=IHC for this family. No strict separation; ordinary unpointed Top HI>IHC still OPEN. Equivariant homotopy interleavings have major prior-art in Halder–Sau–Sen 2026.
8. **Robustness overstatement:** sup norm of orbitwise cell birth function on SAME K leads to immediate h-inclusions; does NOT solve perturbations of attaching maps, covering actions, combinatorics, geometry or noisy point-cloud reconstructions.
9. **Primary collision veto:** perfect-derived ghosts are classical over nonregular rings (Lockridge 2007, Hovey–Lockridge–Puninski 2007), finite equivariant HI universality/Whitehead and stability are established in Sep–Oct 2026 Halder–Sau–Sen, equivariant cohomology (Adams et al 2024), equivariant persistence complexity (Daundkar et al 2026). Showing examples is not a new detection or reconstruction theorem.
10. **No general nonexistence:** failure to achieve a new classification in this session does not prove that no novel theorem exists or that these examples are previously printed word for word. The STOP is a publication-strategy and novelty-gate decision, not an impossibility theorem.
