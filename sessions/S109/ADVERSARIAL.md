# S109 ADVERSARIAL — geometric, categorical and novelty red-team

1. **Triple suspension shift.** The missing cell is T×Δ² attached by T×∂Δ². Quotient is S²∧T_+, not Σ²T with a chosen point of T. For T=pt this must be S²; for T=∅ this must be contractible. Coefficient-wise H̃₂ is ordinary H₀(T), not reduced H̃₀(T). The plus-basepoint convention is essential.
2. **Cofiber vs attaching morphism.** The equivalence hocofib(B1_+→X_+)≃Σ²T_+ is *not* a classification of the map B1→X. An unstable extension or homotopy fibre could depend on coherence beyond T; no universal all-operations no-go claimed.
3. **True cover geometry.** At all positive grades both covers have exactly the same union X and each of their three indexed members is a distinct **proper** CW subcomplex. Cover A consists of three trees meeting pairwise at the three distinct vertices and with empty triple. Cover B consists of L plus separately assigned pendant trees; pairwise and triple overlaps exactly L. Covers are not artificial inconsistent S108 folded squares, not duplicate-set covers, and no noninjective death maps occur.
4. **Index boundaries.** For x<0 or y<0, X and all cover pieces are ∅. At x=0,y=0 enter K with permanent leaves; x=1 and y=1 add separate new leaves, respectively. Each monotone t≤s induces genuine inclusion, and negative-to-positive maps cannot be reverse exits. Both x and y have independent live nonconstant events. No finite-support zero end-map shortcut.
5. **Cover dependence not a union invariant.** Identical union bifunctors mean strict dHI=0. Cover-relative D_B retains nonzero H̃₂ under every positive-quadrant structure map, while D_A is pointwise contractible. Thus no finite diagonal-shift *homology* interleaving exists for the defects. This does NOT contradict stability theorems requiring compatible cover maps, controlled noise or same good-cover class: our cover choices intentionally differ drastically.
6. **Product/good-cover trap.** A good cover with nonempty pairwise overlaps, empty triple has ∂Δ² nerve; filled triple has Δ² nerve; primary H₁ already distinguishes the two *within this special all-contractible overlap class*. Not a general proof that any two non-good triples are primary-visible. Contractible products preserve, arbitrary products not automatically.
7. **Natural model choice.** We calculate the cofiber of the *canonical homotopy Čech blowup*, not the raw non-derived colimit of an incomplete intersection diagram. CW cofibrations make the simplex attachment honest. All whole-diagram objectwise weak equivalences preserve this homotopy cofiber when applied to the full arrow B1→X.
8. **Mapping torus automorphism** φ(a)=a[b,c], b,c fixed is invertible by multiplying a with commutator inverse; on cellular H₁ it is identity. Free group centralizer argument is independent of the H₁ calculation. Note f need not be a graph *homeomorphism*; mapping torus of a graph homotopy equivalence is a finite CW complex, so the F₃⋊Z π₁ calculation remains correct.
9. **Mapping torus center proof.** Any central (g,k) centralizes b and c, hence g=1 because free groups have cyclic centralizer for each nontrivial element, and b,c generate distinct maximal cyclic subgroups. Then φ^k(a)=a[b,c]^k implies k=0. M_id has central stable-letter Z, so π₁ distinguishes them. Identical integral H groups do NOT prove identical ring or primary local-system persistence; none is claimed.
10. **Novelty.** Triple-cell quotient is skeletal bar, good-cover nerve is old, source 2023 functorial nerve and 2024 ε-filtered MVSS preempt generic claims; IA mapping torus blind spot is classical. No HHA first-paper theorem/significance cleared and no original quantity faked.
