# S027 mathematics: a uniform three-distance cofiber-Hopf family

## T62: unbounded integral mixed-cofiber product invisible to pointwise group/ring persistence
**Theorem (internal, priority NOT certified).** For every integer n>=1 there are a positive integer N=N(n), two finite sets of N weighted points P_0,P_n in Euclidean R^(N-1), and radius/height Vietoris–Rips bifiltrations F_0,F_n: (R²,<=)->Top such that:

(i) all points in each cloud have heights 0 or 1; there are exactly three off-diagonal distance values, and the real plane has at most 4 radius strata times 3 height strata (12 product strata);

(ii) the full pointwise Z-graded integral cohomology-*ring* persistence diagrams are naturally isomorphic, including H^0 idempotent/component restriction maps; their integral homology, pi_0 component diagrams, and all componentwise pi_j diagrams (j>=1, with compatible basepoints on persistent connected components) are isomorphic;

(iii) a common rectangle of parameters has derived total cofibers of types (S² vee S⁴) vee (vee^14 S¹), and Cofib(f_n) vee (vee^14 S¹), where f_n:S³->S² has Hopf invariant n. In both cases H² and H⁴ are Z; with their generators x and y, x cup x=0 in the first and x cup x=n y in the second (sign convention/orientations fixed up to global ±);

(iv) for epsilon=1/(100N), d_HI(F_0,F_n)>=epsilon/16=1/(1600N)>0, using the honest homotopy-interleaving definition and natural zigzags of complete diagrams. This is a lower bound, not an exact metric computation.

The coefficient n is unbounded as n varies; the N and ambient Euclidean dimension MAY grow with n. The theorem does NOT give fixed-N or dimension<=5, an algorithmic optimality claim, or source-cleared journal originality.

### Proof 1 — arbitrary Hopf coefficient represented simplicially
The Hopf invariant identifies pi_3(S²) with Z: choose a continuous based representative h_n:S³->S² with Hopf invariant n (e.g. standard Hopf generator precomposed with a degree-n map S³->S³). Choose a finite combinatorial PL triangulation K of S³, and subdivide it sufficiently that both h_n and the constant map h_0 have simplicial approximations f_n,f_0 from ONE COMMON subdivided K to the FIXED 2-sphere Q=boundary of the tetrahedron on {a,b,c,d}. Simplicial approximation preserves homotopy class, hence Hopf invariant. Let v be any vertex of this final PL sphere. The closed star Bplus=st_K(v) and the closure Bminus of its complementary tetrahedra are PL 3-balls, with intersection A=lk_K(v) a PL 2-sphere (PL Schoenflies for a locally flat vertex-link sphere). Their face sets partition K and meet along A. This supplies a finite effective-in-principle construction, but neither N(n) nor a concrete f_2 face list is computed in S027.

### Proof 2 — full-cylinder simplicial diagram
For i=0,n, set C_i=P(K)_s disjoint-union P(Q)_t with order inclusions within each tagged face poset and mixed comparison sigma_s<tau_t iff f_i(sigma) subseteq tau. Simplicial maps make this a genuine transitive poset. Let D_i=Delta(C_i). Its 1-skeleton comparability graph is flag, since mutually comparable elements form chains. The monotone map r:C_i->P(Q), source face sigma to f_i(sigma), target face tau to tau, obeys rj=id and id<=jr, so D_i is homotopy equivalent to sd(Q)=S², and the source inclusion sd(K)->D_i represents f_i.

On the shared N=|P(K)|+14 labelled vertices set E_short to pairs of comparable source faces both lying in Bminus. Let E_high,i be all pairs comparable in C_i. Clearly E_short subset E_high,i. Prescribe squared distances 1-epsilon on E_short, 1 on E_high,i\E_short, 1+epsilon on the other off-diagonal pairs, and 0 on the diagonal. For J=I-11^T/N the centered Gram matrix is G_i=-J D_i² J/2. Its equilateral baseline is J/2. The entrywise perturbation matrix E has |E_ab|<=epsilon and ||E||_2<=(N-1)epsilon<1/100, so on 1-perp, G_i>=(1/2-(N-1)epsilon/2)I>0; rank exactly N-1. Thus a real algebraic Cholesky factorization gives exact Euclidean R^(N-1) point coordinates with those distances. Triangle inequalities follow from the PSD Gram realization; none are assumed. Put height 0 on P(Bplus)_s and height 1 on all remaining labelled vertices, including the 14 target ones.

Set r0=sqrt(1-epsilon/2), r1=sqrt(1+epsilon/2), t0=1/2,t1=3/2. Then the literal four bifiltration complexes are:
L (r0,t0): sd(A) together with isolated low source faces outside Bminus;
B (r1,t0): sd(Bplus), contractible;
C (r0,t1): sd(Bminus) together with isolated source faces outside Bminus and 14 isolated target faces;
D_i (r1,t1): Delta(C_i), homotopy S².
All equalities are combinatorial and independent of unverified dimension reduction. Their inclusions satisfy B intersect C=L and B union C=sd(K) disjoint-union 14 isolated target vertices, denoted U. Therefore the *derived* total cofiber of this inclusion square is the homotopy cofiber U->D_i, equivalent to Cofib(f_i) vee (vee^14 S¹). The 14 disconnected points contribute 14 circles, not 13, because U has 15 components including the source K.

### Proof 3 — every separate pointwise group/ring persistence diagram agrees
The complete bifiltration uses exactly the three distance event values sqrt(1-epsilon),1,sqrt(1+epsilon), and two height events 0,1. At each radial/height stratum: before short radius all present vertices are isolates; at short LOW there is precisely one S² component sd(A) with all other low vertices isolated; at short ALL there is one contractible component sd(Bminus) and all other vertices isolated; at middle LOW Bplus is contractible; at middle ALL D_i homotopy S² and connected; and at largest radius every present-vertex complex is a simplex. These descriptions are COMMON to i=0,n on exactly the same labelled set and same Bplus/Bminus.

The path-component partitions and transition maps are literally equal: at middle ALL every vertex connects via its associated target face, and short edges are common. Nontrivial higher pi_j and positive-degree cohomology can appear only in short LOW sd(A) and middle ALL D_i, both homotopy S²; both persist as identity within their own constant strata. Every map from short LOW to middle ALL factors through middle LOW Bplus, which is contractible (also through short ALL on its non-isolated main component), and therefore induces ZERO on all positive-degree cohomology/homology and componentwise pi_j. There are no other cross-stratum positive-degree maps. H^0 as a ring and all its contravariant structure maps are determined by the identical component mergers. All positive-degree cup products at any *point* vanish for dimensional reasons: only S² components occur, with H^4=0. Choosing generators on the two identity plateaux yields the claimed natural graded integral ring-diagram and higher group-diagram isomorphisms. This does NOT assert a natural weak equivalence between space-valued functors, and pi_j of disconnected spaces are understood componentwise with explicit basepoint choices.

### Proof 4 — integral product and quantitative metric
The standard Hopf-cofiber theorem says that for f:S³->S² of Hopf invariant H(f), the mapping cone has H²=Z x, H⁴=Z y and x²=H(f)y, up to orientation sign. Appending 14 circles does not affect degrees 2 or 4. The constant map gives zero, and f_n gives n. Thus the derived cofibers have inequivalent integral graded rings for all n>=1. This ring calculation is a *topological deduction* from the classical theorem, NOT the independent integral AW chain calculation requested by S027 primary C.

For the quantitative bound put delta=epsilon/16. Since epsilon<1, with r0<1 we have 1-r0=(epsilon/2)/(1+r0)>epsilon/4>2delta=epsilon/8. Also sqrt(1+epsilon)-r1=(epsilon/2)/(sqrt(1+epsilon)+r1)>epsilon/8=2delta since the denominator<4. Finally 2delta<1/2 ensures neither selected height crosses 0 or1. Thus each corner q of the chosen rectangle and q+delta,q+2delta remains within the identical stratum, for BOTH bifiltrations. If a homotopy delta-interleaving existed via natural objectwise weak-equivalence replacements F',G', then the two interleaving identities and naturality (the corrected S026 proof) force each comparison map on the plateau corners to be invertible in Ho(Top), and NATURAL over the complete coherent square. The two derived total cofibers would be weakly equivalent and their integral cohomology rings isomorphic: impossible because one square vanishes and the other has coefficient n. Every delta'<=delta likewise impossible. Hence d_HI>=1/(1600N). QED.

## T63 auxiliary: unbounded separation with uniformly bounded combinatorial strata
The family gives no functional bound on the absolute integral cofiber cup-square structure coefficient using only the complete pointwise integral graded ring and componentwise pi_j persistence diagrams, even restricting to two height events and three off-diagonal distance events, allowing the size N to depend on the desired coefficient. Precisely: for every M>0 one pair as above has the SAME complete pointwise invariant diagrams but mixed-cofiber coefficient discrepancy >=M (choose n>=M). It does not claim all pairs share one globally fixed abstract pointwise diagram as N varies; each paired construction uses a common K_N. This quantifier distinction is essential.
