# S035 — intermediate homotopy-category interleaving distance: a Toda obstruction

**Status:** T75/T76 INTERNAL MATHEMATICAL PROOFS, pending independent referee and complete novelty/priority audit. This dossier is not a formally certified theorem, a journal paper or external proof review. The construction uses a classical Toda example credited to Lanari–Scoccola and Toda/Blumberg–Lesnick. The contribution attempted is its **additional detection by d_IHC**, not discovery of the classical bracket or the d_HI > d_HC example. The prepublication v1 of LS explicitly suggested this adaptation; priority cannot be claimed until separately cleared.

## Definitions, all quantifiers and scope

Use compactly generated spaces with weak homotopy equivalences (equivalently simplicial sets); persistence means a covariant `R`-diagram. Work in the projective model structure and write `F^eps(t)=F(t+eps)`, and `eta_F^{2eps}:F->F^{2eps}` for structure maps. An eps-interleaving in ANY one of the three relevant categories requires morphisms `phi:F->G^eps` and `psi:G->F^eps`, natural in all t, satisfying **both** `psi^eps phi=eta_F^{2eps}` AND `phi^eps psi=eta_G^{2eps}`. d_HC uses `Ho(Top)^R`; d_IHC uses `Ho(Top^R)`; d_HI allows objectwise-weak-equivalence replacements admitting a strict interleaving. Each distance is an infimum over every real eps>=0, NOT existence at an unexamined endpoint. The standard order is d_HC<=d_IHC<=d_HI.

Let `eta_3:S^4->S^3` denote the suspension of the Hopf map, with degree-two pointed maps on both ends. Choose actual pointed maps
`
X0=S4 --f=2--> X1=S4 --g=eta3--> X2=S3 --h=2--> X3=S3.
`
The composites gf and hg are null in the unpointed as well as pointed homotopy categories. The associated Toda bracket `<h,g,f> subset [Sigma S4,S3] = pi5(S3)` **does not contain zero**, as the original Lanari–Scoccola Proposition3.12 explicitly uses this example (ultimately classical Toda). This is an externally SOURCE-STATED input, not a fresh computation of homotopy groups.

Define `F:R->Top`: F(t)=empty for t<0, F(t)=Xi for i<=t<i+1 (i=0,1,2,3), and F(t)=point for t>=4. Every map within a stage is identity, across stages is the prescribed composite, empty initial/point final maps unique. Let `G:R->Top` be empty for t<0 and point for t>=0. All nonempty nonterminal F values are simply connected **finite CW complexes**. At t=0,1 and 1<=eps<3/2,
`
 F(0)=X0,  F(1)=X1,   F(2eps)=X2,   F(1+2eps)=X3,
 G(eps)=G(1+eps)=point.
`
The two-arrow restriction `F|{0->1}=A=(X0 -f-> X1)` and `F^{2eps}|{0->1}=B=(X2 -h-> X3)` has canonical structure morphism
`
 theta=(gf,hg): A -> B,
`
a strictly commuting natural square because `h(gf)=(hg)f`. Zero/null vertical components DO NOT imply zero as a morphism in the homotopy category of arrow diagrams.

## T75: derived-arrow Toda detection (internal proof)

**Lemma.** In the above simply connected sphere example, the morphism `theta` is nonzero in `Ho(Top^[1])`, even though both component maps gf and hg are zero in `Ho(Top)`.

**Proof.** Model spaces by Kan complexes or fibrant compactly generated spaces. For arrows A=(A0->A1), B=(B0->B1), the derived mapping space of the free arrow diagram is the homotopy pullback
`
 RMap(A0,B0) x^h_{RMap(A0,B1)} RMap(A1,B1),
`
where arrows are postcomposition by `h:B0->B1` and precomposition by `f:A0->A1`. One proof: take an objectwise-fibrant B and a projectively cofibrant arrow replacement QA (source QA0 cofibrant and its single latching arrow a cofibration), compute enriched natural transformations, then identify its homotopy type via homotopy invariance of the mapping-space pullback. The arrow category [1] is freely generated by one morphism; this is its full homotopy-coherence datum. A point is a pair of maps `u:A0->B0`, `v:A1->B1` and a path `h u ~ v f` in `RMap(A0,B1)`.

For theta take `u=gf`, `v=hg`, and the constant comparison path at `hgf`. Its component maps are null, so choose nullhomotopy paths `H0:gf~0`, `H1:hg~0`. A path in the derived *arrow* mapping space from theta to the constant arrow-map would force a compatible 2-dimensional filling between `h H0` and `H1 f`. Their difference is the traditional triple Toda obstruction in `pi1 RMap_*(S4,S3)=pi5(S3)`, modulo the standard post-`h` and pre-`f` indeterminacy. As H0,H1 vary, the set of possible differences is **exactly** the Toda bracket `<h,g,f>` under the standard suspension/cylinder convention. Thus theta null in Ho(Top_*^[1]) would imply zero lies in that bracket, contrary to the LS classical sphere example.

We need **unpointed** Top, not merely Top_*. For each relevant `Map(S4,S3)`, the evaluation fibration `Map_*(S4,S3)->Map(S4,S3)->S3` induces isomorphisms on pi0 and pi1, since pi1(S3)=pi2(S3)=0. It therefore cannot destroy this path-component obstruction or the allowed path/loop indeterminacy. The same homotopy-pullback argument applies unpointedly, so theta is nonzero in `Ho(Top^[1])`. QED.

**Logical caution:** a square commuting in `Ho(Top)^[1]` is *strictly weaker* than theta being null in `Ho(Top^[1])`. No appeal is made to the unsupported proposition that separate edge nullhomotopies automatically cohere.

## T76: quantitative separation (internal proof)

**Theorem.** For the above F,G,
`
 d_HC(F,G)=1,  3/2 <= d_IHC(F,G) <= d_HI(F,G) <= 2.
`
Consequently `d_IHC != d_HC` for persistent simply connected finite-CW stages, **provided the standard model-categorical arrow lemma and the sourced Toda input are accepted**. No decision is made here about `d_HI != d_IHC`.

**Proof of HC upper bound.** In `Ho(Top)^R`, use the canonical maps to G shifted by one and point selections from G to F shifted by one (all positive stages are connected). The Y-side composite equals its canonical empty/point structure map. At every t, the F-side composite is a constant morphism, and the required `F(t)->F(t+2)` map is constant in the homotopy category: the ONLY nonterminal pairs are X0->X2 = gf and X1->X3 = hg; both are null. Thus both *natural* interleaving identities hold at each t, and d_HC<=1. This is also the content of the original LS Proposition3.10(1).

**HC lower bound.** If eps<1, then F(0)->F(2eps) is identity on S4 when 2eps<1, and the degree-two S4 map when 1<=2eps<2. Both are nonzero in Ho(Top). But any hypothetical eps-interleaving factors that map through G(eps)=point, contradiction. Thus d_HC>=1 and equality holds.

**IHC lower bound.** Fix 1<=eps<3/2 and suppose there is an eps-interleaving in `Ho(Top^R)`. Restrict the **first** composite `psi^eps phi=eta_F^{2eps}` to the full two-object subposet {0<1}. Derived restriction is well-defined on homotopy categories because it preserves objectwise weak equivalences. The source arrow is A, the target arrow is B. Since `G^eps|{0<1}` is the constant point arrow, the RHS theta factors in `Ho(Top^[1])` as A->point-arrow->B. Maps from a point-arrow to B form a connected derived mapping space on pi0: since 0 is initial in [1], its homotopy limit is B0=X2=S3, which is connected. Hence every such factorization is the null arrow morphism. T75 says theta is NOT null. Contradiction.

If eps<1, standard monotonicity of interleavings in `Ho(Top^R)` upgrades eps to a 1-interleaving by composing with canonical shifts; this too is impossible. Therefore **no eps<3/2** occurs in d_IHC's defining set, so its infimum is at least 3/2. This explicitly accounts for *all real eps*, both compositions (the HC construction checks both; the IHC disproof needs only one) and arrow naturality.

**Upper bound.** The original Lanari–Scoccola rectification theorem gives d_HI<=2 d_HC for 1-parameter persistent spaces; d_IHC<=d_HI holds by definition. Thus d_IHC<=d_HI<=2. Neither endpoint attainment nor d_HI>d_IHC is asserted. QED.

## Independent hostile checks / boundaries

* **Source-input test:** LS Proposition3.10/3.12 gives the zero adjacent composites and nonvanishing bracket; using only Proposition3.12 to infer d_IHC>d_HC would be circular. T75 supplies the distinct arrow-derived mapping argument.
* **Zero arrow versus zero components:** the full homotopy pullback path/2-cell is the missing datum. Simply counting homotopy group ranks or pointwise nulls would yield a false argument.
* **Unpointed countercheck:** ordinary vs based Map pi0/pi1 coincide for target S3 because it is 2-connected; this explicitly addresses the incorrect assumption that based obstruction automatically persists unbased.
* **Factor-through-point countercheck:** point-arrow->(S3->S3) has homotopy classes pi0(S3)=singleton; for disconnected targets this argument must be reconsidered.
* **Full real indexing:** for any eps in [1,3/2) the actual stages at t=0,1 remain (X2,X3), not just integer eps=1. At eps=3/2 the target arrow changes to (X3->point): our argument does NOT forbid that endpoint.
* **Existing upper bound:** use journal-final 2 rather than obsolete original arXiv v1 factor 5. No numerical upper claim more precise than 2.
* **Alternative philosophy:** in an infinity-category an interleaving in Ho(diagrams) can fail even when the pointwise Ho-diagram interleaves, because two nullhomotopies can carry a Toda incompatibility. This is not the failed multi-parameter rectification Proposition3.14; all diagrams here are one-real-parameter.
* **Novelty:** the Toda construction and suggestion to adapt it to intermediate distance already appear in LS preprint v1; proof-level published 2023 and newer literature crosscheck still required. Do NOT pronounce a globally unsolved 2026 question definitively settled/priority won without independent scrutiny.
