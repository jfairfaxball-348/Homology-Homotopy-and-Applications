# S103 MATHEMATICS — a two-transition Moore–Hopf filler ambiguity

**Status:** T256–T258 internal rigorous classical stable-homotopy arguments. The proposed preferred secondary class is **FALSE as a bare-diagram invariant**. There is a legitimate two-component *space of choices*, but no new HHA theorem or quantitative interleaving gap is proved.

## Fixed finite CW data and actual two nonzero transitions

Fix an integer `n >= 5`. Work with compact based CW complexes and based maps. Put
`A=S^(n+1)`, `B=S^(n+1)`, `C=S^n`. Define `f:A->B` to be the based degree-2 map and `g:B->C` a representative of the stable Hopf map `eta_n`. Both are NONNULL: `f` has degree two on integral top homology and `g` represents the generator `pi_(n+1)(S^n)=Z/2`. Nevertheless `gf = eta_n o 2` is nullhomotopic because `2 eta_n=0`. The composite is *not assumed literally equal* to the constant map.

The mapping cone `M=C_f=S^(n+1) union_f e^(n+2)` is the finite Moore CW complex `M(Z/2,n+1)`. A based extension `h:M->S^n` of `g`, with `h|_B=g` strictly, is specified by a based nullhomotopy of `gf` on the top cone. Such extensions exist. Only a CHOSEN nullhomotopy supplies an `h`; the data `(A,f,B,g,C)` do not include one.

## T256: exact two-component nullhomotopy moduli (PROGRAMME_PROVED INTERNAL)

Let `N(f,g)` be the derived path space in `Map_*(A,C)` from `gf` to the constant based map. This is nonempty because `gf\simeq *`. A choice of one path (not canonical) makes `N(f,g)` weakly equivalent to the loop space `Omega Map_*(A,C)`, hence its components are a torsor for
`pi_1 Map_*(S^(n+1),S^n;*) = pi_(n+2)(S^n)=Z/2`.
Here the last equality uses the stable range `n>=5` and the classical stable stem `pi_2^S=Z/2` generated by `eta_n eta_(n+1)`. Thus there are **exactly two components**, not a distinguished nonzero component. Homology, ordinary cup rings, Steenrod operations on stalks, and both arrows are identical for the two choices, because the same diagram underlies both.

**Derived full-diagram invariance, with correct qualifiers:** the *unpointed weak homotopy type* of this homotopy-fiber/path space is functorial up to coherent equivalence for equivalences of the arrow-pair in the homotopy theory of based CW diagrams: derived mapping spaces preserve equivalences and the composite is respected coherently. This does NOT select a base component; a zigzag may re-identify the torsor differently. It also does NOT prove a global stability or Lipschitz bound for arbitrary real interleavings.

## T257: the two choices genuinely yield distinct cone extensions

Apply the stable cohomotopy exact sequence associated to the cofibration
`S^(n+1) --2--> S^(n+1) -> M -> S^(n+2)`.
The pertinent segment is
`[Sigma B,S^n]^S --(Sigma f)^*--> [Sigma A,S^n]^S --q^*--> [M,S^n]^S --i^*--> [B,S^n]^S --f^*--> [A,S^n]^S`.
Both outer stable stem groups at degree one are `Z/2`, and `f^*` acts by 2, hence is zero, so an extension of `eta_n` exists. At degree two `[Sigma A,S^n]^S=pi_2^S=Z/2`; `(Sigma f)^*` again multiplies by 2 and is zero. Exactness makes `q^*:Z/2 -> [M,S^n]^S` INJECTIVE. Therefore, if `h_0` extends `eta_n`, then `h_1=h_0 + q^*(eta_n eta_(n+1))` is a second extension, distinct as a stable class and with the same restriction `g` to `B`.

These are also distinct as *unstable based homotopy classes* `[M,S^n]_*`, because `dim M=n+2 <= 2n-2` for `n>=4` and the Freudenthal suspension comparison is bijective in this range. The stable-group notation `+` is used only in the stable range, not illicitly on arbitrary unstable homotopy sets. This proves that the filler ambiguity is geometrically real, not a formal artefact of choosing words.

## T258: precise counterexample to a canonical chosen-filler persistence invariant

**Proposed false statement P103:** Given a finite CW `[2]` diagram with `gf\simeq *`, its mapping-cone extension of `g` defines **a unique based homotopy class** determined by the undecorated diagram, thus a well-defined secondary invariant under all whole-diagram objectwise weak equivalences.

The *same* fixed diagram `S^(n+1) --2--> S^(n+1) --eta_n--> S^n` has two nonhomotopic extensions `h_0,h_1` (T257). The identity WE of this exact diagram does not discriminate a selected class. Therefore the uniqueness/well-definedness assertion is FALSE. If a filler is explicitly appended as extra data, it becomes a different, decorated problem; treating it as an invariant of the original bare diagram is invalid.

For a literal whole-real persistence diagram, take `D_t=*` for `t<0`; `S^(n+1)` on `0<=t<1`; `S^(n+1)` on `1<=t<2`; `S^n` on `2<=t<3`; and `*` for `t>=3`. Internal maps are identities, maps crossing times 1 and 2 are `f,g`, and crossing 3 are the based collapse; define all remaining maps by composition. This is a strict functor `R->Top_*`, all stalks finite CW and both transitions essential. The two *choices* of nullhomotopy do not produce two different `D` at all, so `d_HI(D,D)=d_IHC(D,D)=0` including both interleaving identities and arbitrary objectwise WE zigzags: the identity strict 0-interleaving already works. No claimed positive metric gap survives, and no phantom clock effect is reported.

Integral homology: at crossing 1 the only nonzero positive-degree map is degree 2 in `H_(n+1)`; at crossing 2 the `eta_n` map has zero homology map due to dimension mismatch. Every sphere stalk has trivial positive-degree cup products and Steenrod squares in its one nonzero reduced cohomological degree. These are the *same for the two extra filler choices*, not a claim that the degree-two transition is invisible to integral persistence. The moduli torsor is a genuine higher homotopy *of the arrow pair*, but the class of one member is not a new undecorated persistence invariant.
