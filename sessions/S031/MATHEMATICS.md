# S031 mathematics: conditional T66 referee and exact Smith–Koszul certificate

## T67: finite exact certificate for *fieldwise* block decomposition
Let P be a finite product of n finite chains (axes lengths at least 2). Let M:P -> Free_fg(Z) be a functor with integer matrices for every comparable pair, commuting on composites. For every order-theoretic k-cube C with 2 <= k <= n (vary k axes between two ordered values, fix other coordinates), let K_C(M) be the bounded Koszul complex, free over Z, with K_i in degrees 0 through k and differential d_i:K_i->K_{i-1}. The following are equivalent:
(A) M tensor Q and M tensor F_p are block-decomposable, with the recursive LLO block class, for *every* prime p.
(B) For all those cubes C, integral H_i(K_C(M))=0 for each 1<=i<=k-1, and H_0(K_C(M)) is torsion-free.
(C) For all C, every nonzero Smith invariant of each d_1,...,d_k is 1, and rank_Q(d_i)+rank_Q(d_{i+1})=rank_Z(K_i) for all 1<=i<=k-1.
For n=1 the cube list is empty, and the (fieldwise) interval theorem makes (A) automatic. This criterion is EXACT FINITE but not succinctly polynomial in n; it does not test map-composite purity unless that is separately supplied.

PROOF. LLO original arXiv:2402.16624v4 Theorem14 equates field block decomposition and vanishing of H_i(K_C(M) tensor field) for 0<i<k over every k-cube. Each K_C(M) is a bounded free integral complex, so UCT supplies split (noncanonically) short exact sequences 0 -> H_i(K;Z) tensor F_p -> H_i(K;F_p) -> Tor_1^Z(H_{i-1}(K;Z),F_p) -> 0, and Q detects free rank. If field homology vanishes for Q and *every* F_p, each middle integral H_i is zero (finite generation), and for i=1 the Tor term detects *every* torsion prime of H0; so H0 must be torsionfree. Conversely those conditions force all field middle homology to vanish. This proves A iff B. B gives purity of each image im d_j: for j=1, H0=coker d1 free; for j>=2, im d_j=ker d_{j-1} since H_{j-1}=0, and every kernel of a map between free Z-modules is saturated. Exactness of each middle degree gives the rational rank equations. Conversely if each im d_j is saturated and rank sums fill each middle K_i, the inclusion im d_{i+1} subset ker d_i between saturated sublattices of equal rank is equality. Im d1 is saturated, making H0 free. Therefore B iff C. All claims concern *one explicitly expanded finite diagram*; Smith algorithms may face exponentially many cubes under succinct input.

## T68: conditional single integral splitting algorithm
Add H1: every comparable composite M(x<=y) has torsion-free cokernel. By S030-T66 (internally proved, referee not externally certified), H1 plus T67(A) gives a common integral decomposition into Z[B] blocks. S031 rederived the algebra as follows. Supports of the recursive LLO family are U_u, D_v (duplicated full support removed), and axis bands [a,c] with 1<=a<=c<=m_i-2; count 2|P|-1+sum_i (m_i-2)(m_i-1)/2. For v<t the simultaneous kernel L_v at bottom of the maps to a_j=(v_j+1)e_j (axes with v_j<max) retains *exactly* D_w with w<=v. The image in M(v) is D_v alone and is pure because its rank agrees over Q and all F_p. An integral section to L_v and projection from M(v) split the D_v functor naturally. For an exit y not <=v, the chosen a_j <=y kills the inclusion, even when other coordinates of y are low. No downset has an incoming map from outside.

After all proper deaths, let e=e_i^a with all other coordinates zero, h=h_i^c with all others maximal, l at coordinate a-1 and all others maximal, and t top. For fixed i peel c descending and a ascending. Define K=ker(M(e->t)), H=im(K->M(h)), I=im(M(l->h)). Under the field-block hypothesis and previous peel order, K->M(h) has only the current band (a,c) as image, and I consists of different block coordinates. Their integer images and sum H+I are saturated by Q/all-Fp rank agreement and the Smith unit criterion. A single integer q:M(h)->H can satisfy q|H=id and q|I=0. For each supported x define inclusion M(e->x)s and retraction q M(x->h). For any incoming x_i<a, x<=l and retraction kills its composite. For any outgoing y_i>c, M(e->y)s=0 already over Q and hence integrally because all remaining K contributions die at c. Internal arrows preserve identity, yielding one natural summand with integral section/retraction. Repeating across axes preserves H1 and field decompositions. The residual consists only of principal upsets: increasing linear-extension birth bases, with each latching image pure under all-field rank tests, recover a coherent integral representable splitting (the classical S011-T29 step).

Multiplicity rank inversion, including boundary: after deaths F_i(q,s)=rank(e_i^q->h_i^s)-rank(e_i^q->t)=sum_{a<=q<=s<=c} mu_{i,a,c}, where s<=m_i-2 and a,c interior; F_i(0,s)=F_i(q,m_i-1)=0. Then mu_{i,a,c}=F_i(a,c)-F_i(a-1,c)-F_i(a,c+1)+F_i(a-1,c+1), valid for unequal axes. Smith purity of all *composites* makes all terms prime-independent. No compatible fieldwise bases are assumed. This is a second in-programme audit, not an external universal certification.

## Explicit H0 counter-control: why finite p sampling fails
On the Boolean square put M(bottom)=0, M(two middle vertices)=Z, M(top)=Z^2. The two middle-to-top injections are columns (1,0)^T and (1,2)^T; each comparable map is primitive (the others are zero), so H1 map purity holds. The square Koszul complex has C2=0, C1=Z^2, C0=Z^2, with d1=[[1,-1],[0,-2]]. Its Smith diagonal is (1,2); over Z, H1=0 while H0=Z/2. Over Q or any F_p with p!=2, H1=0, but over F2 its dimension is 1. Thus every composite can be pure and almost every field passes while all-field block decomposition FAILS. This is **not** a T66 counterexample because H2 fails at p=2. It proves that the H0 torsion condition in T67 is indispensable and that no fixed finite prime sample can replace the exact Smith certificate (replace 2 by any prime p).
