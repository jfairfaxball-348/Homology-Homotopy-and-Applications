## C08 independent attacks

1. **Order closure:** c is terminal only within L, not globally. There is no c→Q, so p→c forces no spurious crossing arrow. Crossing p→q is stable under pre/postcomposition. Chains with c and Q cannot exist; the union-of-cone/cylinder proof is literal.
2. **Wrong degeneracy shortcut rejected:** repeated height labels need not mean horizontally degenerate simplices. W_(r,s) has actual nonconstant diagrams. The inequalities w≤J≥ke independently give its nerve contraction. Normalization occurs at E1 after vertical homology; no false two-column chain-level identification is claimed.
3. **Section functor test:** [1]×[q] has commuting square relations. Coordinatewise pairs (p_j,q_j) with F(p_j)≤q_j supply all crossing diagonals via order transitivity, so the arrow-poset identification neither omits nor invents q-simplices.
4. **Topology/filtration distinction:** D retracts onto Q fixing Q; the induced mapping-cone equivalence therefore preserves the image of top-level homology. F0 of the ACTUAL section total complex maps exactly to the two level inclusions. This pins the extension subgroup without claiming a global filtered chain equivalence from the mere cofiber homotopy type.
5. **Explicit K check:** each of the fifteen edges is in exactly two listed faces. The six displayed links are cycles, so no singular vertex is hidden. Euler characteristic one identifies the closed surface as RP²; the separate spanning-tree presentation gives Z/2 and identifies the T456 circle as its generator. Stellar subdivision does not change this circle or homology.
6. **Simplicial vertex-map check:** the three w-faces map to the northern suspension cap. The only nondegenerate remaining images are the three southern faces from 124,135,236. No face contains both n and s. All six cap faces occur once modulo two. The wedge basepoint maps to itself, so f1 is a map on the wedge, not two incompatible maps at the common vertex.
7. **Coefficient parity:** H2(K';Z)=0 does NOT imply the mod-2 H2 map is zero. Cellular chain map u→k v with k odd kills integral homology but detects the Tor term mod 2. Requiring identical section homology over ALL fields would fail for this pair. Exact integral E1/E2 equality is the stated claim.
8. **Abutment without spectral-sequence reasoning:** the cellular cone has degree-2 cycles exactly x=v_B,y=t_A; B u has nonzero d2 and cannot join them. Boundary relations are 2x,kx−2y. Odd k gives x=2y and y of order four, whereas k=0 gives two independent order-two generators. This independently checks the extension calculation. H1 and H3 remain Z/2, so the common page table has no omitted groups.
9. **Rigidity scope:** F comparable to a constant is sufficient for splitting in the constructed class, not necessary. A nullhomotopic continuous map need not admit a single order inequality to a constant. No iff or universal geometrical split theorem is claimed.
10. **Priority:** an explicit simplicial realization of a familiar extension phenomenon can be useful but does not itself pass HHA significance/novelty. VHT's field direct-sum conclusion remains valid.

## C11 independent attacks

- Necessity of T23 checked on ALL seven block supports; a singleton side vertex fails middle exactness, and a three-point L fails in the relevant orientation. The claim concerns blocks, not arbitrary intervals.
- Splitting K at the source is a module splitting because every outgoing arrow annihilates K. Splitting D/J at the target is a module splitting because every incoming arrow lands in J. No general pointwise-to-natural-splitting assumption is used.
- Intersection I=U∩V is primitive in each side because U/I↪D/V and V/I↪D/U, not because arbitrary intersections have primitive sums. The complement argument requires J itself primitive and U+V=J. The proposed determinant-m example violates exactly that requirement.
- Sharpness if individual primitivity is dropped: the Bezout exact square (3,2;2,3) has J=D but U,V nonprimitive. It is exact after every field reduction yet fails integral blocks. This is NOT the separate every-arrow-split counterexample.
- The UCT implication checks H0 torsion, not just H1_Z=0: Tor(H0,Fp) occurs in middle homology of the reduced complex. Thus the all-fields + primitive-arrow diamond rigidity is genuinely proved. It cannot be extrapolated to a 3-cube where several lattice choices must be made simultaneously.
- Geometric graph-only shortcut rejected: two primitive loops with joint index m cannot both be source subgraphs of a graph realizing this top H1 diagram. Mapping cylinders give dimension two and avoid the graph obstruction.

## C07 attacks

- Known 2013 theorem works over a PID and constructs filtered chain maps, so calling the same result a new integral theorem is rejected.
- A pair map is not automatically a map of open complements. For nested compact pairs equality T_t∩X_s=T_s is precisely the inclusion condition; when it holds the inclusion is closed/proper, and the standard BM identification is natural. When it fails there is no requested inclusion at all.
- Same stratum does not imply same filtration index. The matching theorem is sufficient only; no claim that all possible filtered reductions must obey a chosen matching's equality condition.
