# S070 ADVERSARIAL — two hostile constructions and scoped conclusion

## Attack 1: worst possible noncofibrant/nonmonic *whole* G zigzag and uncountable shoelace

An illegitimate proof would replace every G'(t) by 0, presume the original crossings are cofibrations, and factor every 2e-F-arrow into literal zero. Instead take arbitrary whole zigzags to G=0, allow noncofibrant Q and negative t, and apply ONE projective cofibrant replacement of the entire interleaving category \(P_e\). Its cells attach at arbitrary real births with no local finiteness, so the **generator-level** structural-cofibration proof must be transfinite. The map from the G-lane at u−e into F at u is a cofibration in this single replacement, regardless of the original. The functorial pushout by 0 uses left properness, not objectwise homology. Check T184.2 for u<0, 0, e, 5 and u>5: all legal because G=0 identically; no nonempty basepoint is needed. This resolves the stated adversary in the spectra setting. The analogous unpointed Top setting still requires the original source and point-lane quotient, and is **not claimed resolved** by this specialisation.

## Attack 2: nonconventional Toda variants, and weak-equivalence direction

A strict chain with literal consecutive zero maps supplies a five-filler constant defining system and 0 in **Kochman's** *complete* bracket; pairwise nullhomotopic composites alone do not give compatible fillers. An arbitrary zigzag of whole-diagram weak equivalences does NOT furnish a point-set inverse, but restricting to five times furnishes an isomorphism in Ho(Sp)^[4]; the conventional complete bracket depends only on the four homotopy classes and its union over representatives, so zero membership transports. Neither this logic nor 2=2 reversal identifies **all possible modern higher Toda variants** with Xu's unspelled convention. Xu final p1620 cites Kochman's two books in the very sentence reporting the θ4 fourfold brackets; strong evidence, not a literal definition equality. The abstract theorem is therefore phrased **with Kochman's** bracket. The numerical instance is **source-dependent**, not an axiom-free computation of θ4. A putative alternative bracket containing zero would defeat the application unless its Kochman equality is proved, but would not defeat T184.

## Attack 3: derived class versus vertexwise null; projective vs pointwise homotopy

The n=0,1,2 components \(a_1a_0,a_2a_1,a_3a_2\) are zero in Ho(Sp) separately but a coherent null in Ho(Sp^[2]) does not automatically follow. We used a projectively cofibrant chain, two explicit restriction fibrations and **two π1 vanishing corrections**, both π29^S_(2)=0. If π29 were nonzero (e.g. if one silently used the integral rather than 2-primary case), this repair could fail. The derived floor-left-Kan adjunction is proved at objectwise level and maps the integer two-shift map to \(\eta_F^2\) in Ho(Sp^R), NOT merely Ho(Sp)^R. No assertion that all higher naturality obstructions vanish generally.

## Attack 4: a tempting fake weakening to sSet pointed spaces or modules over a field

A bracket in spectra is not automatically a bracket obstruction in ordinary unpointed Top^R: stabilisation only sends zero forward, and IHC interleaving need not transport backwards. We **do not** infer the stronger original theorem. Conversely, the September 2026 equality dHI=dIHC=dHC for dg modules **over a field** does not imply equality for spectra or \(\mathbb Z_{(2)}\)-local homotopy types. This distinction protects scope; it does not prove originality.
