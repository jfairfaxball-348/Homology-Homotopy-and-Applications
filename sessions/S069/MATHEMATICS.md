## S069-T180. Stable-first fourfold ZERO lemma, explicit complete defining-system

Let Z_0 \xrightarrow{f_1} Z_1 \xrightarrow{f_2} Z_2 \xrightarrow{f_3} Z_3 \xrightarrow{f_4} Z_4 be an actual pointed chain of CW spaces with f_{i+1}f_i literally constant for i=1,2,3 (rather than only separately null in Ho). Apply the reduced suspension-spectrum functor and, as needed, 2-localisation. All three consecutive composites in spectra remain literally zero/pointed null, and each *chosen* nullhomotopy can be the constant one. The resulting stable sequence admits a full fourfold composition Toda defining system with **zero output**, as follows.

Kochman's original Chapter 2, printed pp13–16, Definition 2.2.1 (composition product) writes four elementary maps G_{01},G_{12},G_{23},G_{34}. For t=4 the required nonprimitive extensions are exactly five: three first-cone maps G_{02},G_{13},G_{24} (each on one reduced cone parameter) and two second-cone maps G_{03},G_{14} (each on two cone parameters). In Kochman's coordinate-free sphere-spectrum model, for any 0≤i<j−1≤3 other than (0,4), the domain of G_{ij} is D V_{i+1}∧⋯∧D V_{j−1}∧S V_j∧⋯∧S V_4∧S U and its target is S V_{j+1}∧⋯∧S V_4∧E_j U (with structure maps as required). Its boundary condition is

  G_{ij}|_{∂(D V_{i+1}∧⋯∧D V_{j−1}∧S V_j∧⋯)} = ⋃_{k=i+1}^{j−1} G^k_{ij},

where G^k_{ij} is the composite of the lower pieces G_{ik} and G_{kj}, including the interchange/structure map. For j=i+2, the boundary is the adjacent composite G_{i,i+1}G_{i+1,i+2}. Taking this composite to be literally zero, choose each of G_{02},G_{13},G_{24} to be the *constant extension*. For j=i+3, the two boundary faces are G_{i,i+1}G_{i+1,i+3} and G_{i,i+2}G_{i+2,i+3}; both are constant, so choose G_{03},G_{14} constant. The missing final G_{04} is **not filled**; instead Kochman defines the output by the three-face attaching map

  \widetilde G_{04}= (G^1_{04}∪G^2_{04}∪G^3_{04})◦(h_4∧1),

on S(R²+V_1+⋯+V_4)∧S U. The three faces are obtained from the composites G_{01}G_{14}, G_{02}G_{24}, G_{03}G_{34}, with the indicated cone, interchange and suspension identifications. Every face is constant under the choices above, so the output represents **0**. The bracket is the union over *all* representatives/defining systems, hence having one zero defining system implies 0 belongs. Kochman Def 2.2.2 gives the corresponding smash construction and Theorem 2.2.3 equates his composition and smash versions.

**Transport without unstable-bracket convention:** If two simply connected unpointed five-chains are connected by a zigzag of natural objectwise weak equivalences, their [4] chains are isomorphic in Ho(Top_sc)^[4]. By Lanari–Scoccola 2023 Lemma 3.8 the forgetful Ho(Top_sc,*)→Ho(Top_sc) is an equivalence; its functor-on-[4] is too. A pointed lift of the five-chain iso passes under Σ∞ and 2-localisation to an isomorphism of diagrams in Ho(Sp_(2))^[4]. The **complete** stable bracket (union over homotopy classes and all compatible defining systems, rather than a preselected operation) is invariant under whiskering with stable equivalences/isomorphisms: choose cofibrant-fibrant reps, replace objects by equivalences/cofibration cylinders and apply the homotopy extension/left Quillen pushout equivalences; inverse equivalences reverse the inclusion. In particular ZERO membership transfers. We require only the forward zero implication; no claim that every unstable defining system transports or that BBG/BJT operations agree element by element with Kochman. This is more economical than S068.

## S069-T181. Renewed derivation of d_IHC=1

Coherence defects between h u_s and v_s i, and between k v_s and w_s j, are rel-endpoint loops in Map(Q0,D), Map(Q1,E). ## S069-T182. ALL replacements and full uncountable quotient, including the boundary

An ε-homotopy interleaving means arbitrary WHOLE objectwise weak-equivalence zigzags on **both** unpointed diagrams, then strict natural a,b and **both** shifted identities at all real times. Put its lanes and crossings into one shoelace poset P_ε. Apply Sing and one projective cofibrant replacement Q of the *entire* simplicial P_ε-diagram. For every poset arrow, Q's transition is monic: representable projective generating cells born at p map by identity to all q≥p; attaching boundaries preserves mono and retracts preserve monos (Lanari–Scoccola §4 Proposition 4.5). This argument works for a small poset with uncountably many objects because each domain/codomain is a set and the projective cofibrantly generated model is available, not by choosing finitely many time points.

For u≥ε define V_u=im(QG(u−ε)↪QF(u)); because QG(s) is empty for s<0 and weakly contractible nonempty for s≥0, V_u is nonempty weakly contractible and preserved under all later F maps by naturality of the crossing. Form H_F(u)=QF(u)/V_u for u≥ε, and H_F(u)=QF(u) for u<ε. Mixed-time maps are followed by the target quotient. Each QF(u)→H_F(u) is a weak equivalence: V_u↪QF(u) cofibration and V_u→* weak equivalence, so simplicial left properness applies; naturality proves a WHOLE WE, not unrelated pointwise ones. Set H_G(s)=∅ for s<0 and * for s≥0. The born-point crossing H_G(t)→H_F(t+ε) is the collapsed image class, and H_F(t)→H_G(t+ε) the collapse; these are natural. H_F(t)→H_F(t+2ε) is literally constant for all t≥0, because original strict interleaving identities made the QF 2ε composite land inside V_(t+2ε). The G-lane identity is the unique point map; for t<0 all empty-source composites are vacuous, and when t<0<t+2ε the structure map has empty domain.

For 0≤ε<1, any ε-strict interleaving would thicken to ε=1, so it suffices to exclude 1≤ε<5/4 to obtain the *strict lower bound* (the IHC homology bound already excludes ε<1 independently). Let c=2ε−2∈[0,1/2), t=(0,1,2+c,3+c,4+2c). All five lie in their intended half-open [i,i+1), and t_(i+2)=t_i+2ε for i=0,1,2. Restrict H_F to sampled arrows; every adjacent double is **literally** zero. Point it using a basepoint at t0 propagated along arrows; since double composites constant to the collapsed point, the later chosen basepoints agree (for i≥2 propagated basepoint equals quotient point). The sampled whole diagram is objectwise WE zigzag equivalent to the original unpointed five-chain (restriction of WHOLE WEs); S069-T180 gives stable 0 in its conventional bracket after pointed lifting. Under *the same Kochman conventional stable interpretation* for Xu, final Xu's singleton {θ4} excludes zero. Hence, **subject to the explicit conventional-identification and whole-model hypotheses,** dHI≥5/4. ε=5/4 not excluded: t4=5 lies in the terminal point stage, not L40. No exact endpoint is calculated or needed.
