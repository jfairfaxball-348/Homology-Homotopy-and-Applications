## Honest novelty and paper sufficiency
Precise prospective title: *A modular coefficient obstruction to rectifying persistence interleavings*. Candidate theorem: There exists a real persistence diagram F of unbounded cofibrant Ch(F2[C4]) with dCohI(F,0)=1/2, dIHC(F,0)=1, and dHI(F,0)≥5/4, whereas all three become1/2 on forgetting C4-action. Corollary: coefficient forgetfulness strictly decreases both derived and homotopy interleaving distance-to-zero for this F (numerical 1→1/2 and ≥5/4→1/2). This does not imply the published field theorem is wrong and is NOT a topological-space theorem.
