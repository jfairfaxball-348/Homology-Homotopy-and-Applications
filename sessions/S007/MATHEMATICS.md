## S007-T18: exact integral cofiber extension counterexample (PROGRAMME_PROVED, classical, NOT a section-complex theorem)

Let M_m(r)=S^r ∪_m e^(r+1) for m=2. Let A=B=M_2(1) ∨ M_2(2), based finite CW complexes. Their reduced H1 and H2 are Z/2, with all other reduced homology zero. Define f0:A→B to be the constant based map. Define f1 by collapsing the M_2(2) wedge summand; on M_2(1), collapse its 1-skeleton to get S², then include that S² as the 2-skeleton of the M_2(2) summand of B. Both f0 and f1 induce zero maps on every reduced integral homology group.

Explicit oriented reduced cellular chains of A,B: C3=Z[w], C2=Z[u,v], C1=Z[t], d3(w)=2v, d2(u)=2t,d2(v)=0. For f1, chain map f1_2(u)=v_B, f1_2(v)=0, f1_1=f1_3=0. For f0, every reduced cellular chain map vanishes. Each commutes with d, including f1_2 d3=0=d3 f1_3.

Let Cof(f) denote the ordinary based homotopy cofiber; reduced cellular cone chain complex has Cof(f)_n=C_n(B)⊕C_(n−1)(A), d(b,a)=(d_B b+f(a),−d_A a). In degree 2 the cycles are generated by x=(v_B,0) and y=(0,t_A). For f1, the image of d3 in this cycle group is generated by 2x (from w_B) and x−2y (from u_A). Hence H̃2(Cof(f1);Z)=<x,y | 2x=0,x=2y> ≅ Z/4. For f0, relations are only 2x=0 and 2y=0, giving H̃2(Cof(f0);Z)≅(Z/2)⊕(Z/2). All other degree-3 boundaries lie in degree 3, not degree 2, and d2 sends (u_B,0) to 2t_B, so there are no omitted degree-2 cycle generators. This independently proves the two homology values without running a CAS or relying only on a LES.

The cofiber long exact sequences for BOTH maps yield the SAME short exact sequence 0→coker(H2 f)=Z/2→H̃2 Cof(f)→ker(H1 f)=Z/2→0. The extension is nontrivial for f1 and split for f0. Consequently a proposed criterion using only the homology of levels and the induced maps (or these two graded pieces) cannot reconstruct integral cofiber homology. This is classical extension algebra, not a novelty claim. Over a field, the abstract sequence splits but that does not yield an integral splitting.

**CRITICAL BOUNDARY:** Neither cofiber has been proved to be the section-spectrum/filtration abutment for a SPECIFIED finite simplicial height h with prescribed section spaces. A tempting construction is a two-level simplicial homotopy-pushout of a cone(A) ← A → B, using a prism and heights 0,1, but the exact simplicial section space S_h[0,1], degeneracies, natural filtration and E2 pages are NOT calculated. Thus T18 proves a precise *related* geometric fact, NOT an actual C08 two-height counterexample, not a theorem about S_h, and not novelty certified.
