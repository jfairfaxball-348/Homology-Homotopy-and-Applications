## 1. Repair the S025 inference without weakening the theorem
S025 said that if a composite b∘a is a weak equivalence, then a is a weak equivalence "by two-out-of-three". That sentence is FALSE by itself: e.g. an inclusion of a point into two discrete points followed by the collapse of both points to one point has weakly equivalent composite but the inclusion is not a weak equivalence.

Fortunately, the TWO interleaving identities plus plateau naturality repair the argument. Suppose F',G' are strictly δ-interleaved and naturally objectwise weak-equivalent (by zigzags) to F,G, and for every corner q of an ordered rectangle the canonical F(q)→F(q+2δ) and G(q)→G(q+2δ) are weak equivalences. Also all shifts q,q+δ,q+2δ remain within the same literal stratum of each original bifiltration. Naturality of the zigzags and two-out-of-three imply the corresponding structure maps of F',G' for 0→δ→2δ are weak equivalences, hence isomorphisms in Ho(Top).

Write a_q:F'(q)→G'(q+δ), b_q:G'(q)→F'(q+δ). The first interleaving identity b_(q+δ) a_q=F'(q→q+2δ) gives a_q a left inverse *up to an isomorphism*: a_q is a split monomorphism in Ho(Top). The second identity a_(q+δ) b_q=G'(q→q+2δ) shows a_(q+δ) is split epimorphic. Naturality gives
  G'(q+δ→q+2δ) a_q = a_(q+δ) F'(q→q+δ).
The flanking maps are invertible, so a_q is also split epimorphic. In any category a split mono and a split epi coincide as inverses; thus a_q is invertible in Ho(Top), i.e. a weak homotopy equivalence. The four a_q give a NATURAL objectwise weak-equivalence square, and natural zigzags to originals identify the two derived total cofibers. This is the corrected proof, NOT the false S025 one-composite implication.
