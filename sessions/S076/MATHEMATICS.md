This is falsifiable: if the coefficient field alone admits splitting/formality, the full nonzero condition fails. The proof uses an explicit metric clock and arbitrary whole-replacement quantifiers, not merely the definition of a Toda bracket. It does NOT claim that classical fourfold operations themselves are newly invented.
