## T251 — canonical algebraic compatibility homomorphism (PROVED INTERNAL)
For ANY natural transformation M:B=>A of abelian-group diamonds, define
omega_M:E->K11/(image(K10->K11)+image(K01->K11)).
For [a] in E represented by a in A00, the side images a_i of a lie in image M_i by definition of E. Choose lifts b10 in B10, b01 in B01 with M_i(b_i)=a_i. Define
 omega_M([a]) = [ B(10->11)(b10) - B(01->11)(b01) ].
Naturality makes the difference lie in K11, since the A11 images of a agree. Changing b_i by an element of K_i changes the difference by the stated quotient subgroup. Changing a by M00(b00) permits changing b_i by the corresponding B lower images of b00; those cancel in B11 by commutativity. Therefore omega_M is well-defined and additive. When Delta_K is injective, the target is canonically coker lambda_K. This construction does not require a CW cover. It is the algebraically *specified* map, not a freely chosen monomorphism between abstract isomorphic groups.
