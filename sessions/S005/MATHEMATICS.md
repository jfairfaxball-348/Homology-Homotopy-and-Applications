All groups are finitely generated free abelian unless explicitly stated otherwise. ## S005-T12 — 3-dimensional H2 saturation lemma (general elementary topology)

**Lemma.** Let L⊂K be CW subcomplexes of a finite CW complex K of dimension at most 3, with H_3(K;Z)=0 and H_2(K;Z) torsion-free. Then (i) H_3(L)=0; (ii) H_2(L) is torsion-free. More precisely the kernel of H_2(L)→H_2(K) is free, and H_2(L)/ker embeds in free H_2(K).

**Proof.** Cellular inclusions identify C_3(L) with a subgroup generated by a subset of the basis of C_3(K); in degree three there are no cellular 4-boundaries. A 3-cycle in L stays a 3-cycle in K and cannot vanish except trivially, proving injection H_3(L)↪H_3(K)=0. For the pair (K,L), cellular C_4(K,L)=0, whence H_3(K,L)=ker(∂_3:C_3(K,L)→C_2(K,L)) is a subgroup of a finitely generated free abelian group and therefore free. The LES, using H_3(K)=0, gives
0→H_3(K,L)→H_2(L)→H_2(K).
Any torsion in H_2(L) maps to zero in torsion-free H_2(K), and would lie in the free subgroup H_3(K,L), impossible. This proves both assertions. No simply connectedness is used. Note that injectivity of H_2(L)→H_2(K) is **not** claimed: the kernel can be nonzero free.

## S005-T13 — complete A-bottom criterion for graph-admissible B

Let A,B:P→free-Ab be any commuting diagrams and M:B⇒A any natural transformation. Write
δ_A=(a_10,-a_01):A_00→A_10⊕A_01,  D_A=coker δ_A.
Suppose **B is graph-admissible in the precise S004-T8 sense**: both lower B maps split inject and the induced upper B amalgam map λ_B:(B_10⊕B_01)/B_00→B_11 is split inject.

Consider exactly the local topological cover type used in S004: finite based connected, total dimension≤3; X_p=U_1,p∪U_2,p∪U_3,p; U_2,p,U_3,p and all pairwise intersections contractible; triple T_p connected with \tilde H_1(T_p)=B_p and no other reduced homology; U_1,p has \tilde H_2(U_1,p)=A_p and no other reduced homology; labelled filtrations literally meet-exact; the integral natural Mayer–Vietoris d_2 equals M.

**IFF theorem (within B graph-admissible class).** Such a total-3D literal CW cover diamond exists **if and only if** δ_A is a *split injection* (equivalently its image is a primitive sublattice of A_10⊕A_01). Individual A_00→A_10 and A_00→A_01 need NOT be injective, split, primitive, or nonzero. Upper A arrows, M, and rank-zero cases are arbitrary compatible integral homomorphisms. The construction can ensure each U_1,p is simply connected.

**Necessary direction (works for arbitrary B, not only graph-admissible).** Put F=U_1,10∪_{U_1,00} U_1,01, an actual subcomplex of U_1,11. With reduced H_1 and H_3 of the three constituent U_1 spaces zero, integral Mayer–Vietoris gives exactly
H_3(F)≅ker δ_A, H_2(F)≅coker δ_A.
Since dim U_1,11≤3, H_3(U_1,11)=0, H_2(U_1,11)=A_11 free, T12 applied to F⊂U_1,11 forces H_3(F)=0 and H_2(F) torsion-free. Thus δ_A is injective with free cokernel. For homomorphisms between finite free Z-modules this is precisely a split injection. The argument is independent of M, B, contractibility of pairwise overlaps and any assumptions about *mixed-grade* intersection equality. Hence a genuine universal obstruction for this degree-pure 3D cover class.

**Sufficient direction, part 1: graph B.** Apply S004-T8's literal nested rose construction to B. Then T_p is a connected graph, T_10∩T_01=T_00, and the induced integer H_1 maps are exactly B. Put S_p=C_u T_p ∪_{T_p} C_v T_p (unreduced suspension with fixed cone vertices). It is 2-dimensional, simply connected and homotopy equivalent to a wedge of rank(B_p) 2-spheres; suspend the common basepoint to the same contractible segment I at all grades. S_10∩S_01=S_00 strictly. Its H_2 square is B.

**Part 2: arbitrary bottom A arrows, without false split assumptions.** Let E_00=∨^{rank A_00}S². Choose based cellular maps k_10:E_00→E_10=∨^{rank A_10}S² and k_01:E_00→E_01=∨^{rank A_01}S² inducing the *arbitrary* bottom matrices a_10,a_01. Existence follows by pinch/signed-degree sphere maps, including zero maps. Define Y_00=E_00 and Y_10=M_{k_10}, Y_01=M_{k_01}, with two independent mapping-cylinder collars attached to one literal E_00; their intersection is **exactly** Y_00. These finite simply connected spaces have dimension≤3, H_2=A at the three grades and no other reduced homology; their inclusions realise a_10,a_01. Set V_Y=Y_10∪_{Y_00}Y_01. Van Kampen gives π_1(V_Y)=0, and integral MV with δ_A split injection gives
H_3(V_Y)=0, H_2(V_Y)=D_A, a finite free group.

**Part 3: arbitrary upper A arrows in dimension three.** Commutativity gives λ_A:D_A→A_11, not assumed injective or primitive. Pick a Z-basis x_1,...,x_d of H_2(V_Y)=D_A. Let E_11=∨^{rank A_11}S², attached to V_Y at its common basepoint. Since V_Y∨E_11 is simply connected, Hurewicz identifies π_2 with H_2, so for each i a based cellular S²→V_Y∨E_11 represents x_i−λ_A(x_i). Attach one 3-cell by this map for each i. Define Y_11 to be the resulting CW complex. The relative cellular boundary on new 3-cells has H_2-coordinates [I_d;−λ_A], so it is injective; the pair LES implies \tilde H_3(Y_11)=0 and \tilde H_2(Y_11)≅A_11, with the *precisely specified* maps from Y_10 and Y_01. Simply connectedness and dimension≤3 persist. All new cells first occur at 11; Y_10∩Y_01=Y_00 in Y_11. Neither a mapping cylinder on 3-dimensional V_Y (would make 4-cells) nor a claim of homotopy equivalence Y_11≃∨S² is required. If d=0 there are no new 3-cells. The zero upper A case is included.

**Part 4: strict compatible f and the actual three-cover cells.** Start f_00:S_00→Y_00 collapsed on I with H_2 matrix M_00. Since graph B arrows embed bases, at 10/01 retain f_00 **literally** on S_00, choose maps on the new suspended-rose S² generators representing each new M_10/M_01 column in Y_10/Y_01 respectively. Naturality makes old columns automatically equal after passage to side homology. These maps agree on S_00 on the nose. On the top old side union S_10∪S_01 they glue strictly into Y_10∪Y_01⊂Y_11. The graph-admissible upper B latching gives further independent top rose edges. Map their suspended S² generators into simply connected Y_11 representing the remaining M_11 columns via Hurewicz. Because the old columns induce λ_A M_D=M_11 λ_B, this yields f_11:S_11→Y_11 extending both f_10 and f_01 as actual subcomplex restrictions and inducing M_11. Relative cellular approximation can be applied while fixing already assigned subcomplex maps.

With fixed cone tips u,v,w and collars, set P_p=C_u T_p, Q_p=C_v T_p, W_p=C_w S_p,
U_1,p=M_{f_p}, U_2,p=C_w P_p, U_3,p=C_w Q_p,
X_p= M_{f_p}∪_{S_p} W_p.
Exactly as in S004-T11, U_1∩U_2=P, U_1∩U_3=Q, U_2∩U_3=C_w T, and triple intersection=T. All required pair overlaps/U_2/U_3 are contractible. Distinct cylinder collars and wedge/cone cells on incomparable sides ensure every named K_10∩K_01=K_00. There are no preloaded or mixed-grade anticipatory cells in *this construction*. Because T graphs dim1, S dim2, Y dim≤3, the mapping cylinder and the entire union X have **dimension at most 3**. The cofiber LES gives natural 0→\tilde H_3(X)→B—M→A→\tilde H_2(X)→0 and all other reduced total homology zero; the Čech–MV double-complex two-cone transgression gives d_2=M, with one global suspension orientation convention. No dimension-4 cone on a 2D T appears.

Necessity and sufficiency are proved within the explicitly bounded B class. QED.

## S005-T14 — integral examples beyond S004

**Nonprimitive individual arrows but primitive joint arrow.** A_00=A_10=A_01=A_11=Z. The lower A arrows are ×2 and ×3 (neither split), the upper arrows from 10 and 01 are ×3 and ×2, respectively. Thus δ_A(1)=(2,−3), primitive since gcd(2,3)=1, and D_A≅Z. Take B_00=B_10=B_01=B_11=Z with all structure maps identity (graph-admissible). Set M_00=×1, M_10=×2, M_01=×3, M_11=×6. Every naturality square commutes: 2·1=2, 3·1=3, 3·2=2·3=6. Theorem T13 gives a literal total-3D square. At the four grades \tilde H_2(X)=0, Z/2, Z/3, Z/6 respectively and \tilde H_3(X)=0. The cover has nonsplit lower A transitions and genuine torsion whose orders combine at the join. This is *not* covered by S004-T11.

**Saturation kill witness.** A_00=A_10=A_01=Z, bottom A arrows ×2 and ×4. Then δ_A(1)=(2,−4); ker δ_A=0 but D_A≅Z⊕Z/2. There can be no total-3D degree-pure U_1 meet-exact diamond, *for any B or M*, regardless of the upper A diagram. This rules out the incorrect “joint injectivity alone suffices” criterion.

**Joint kernel kill witness.** A_00=Z, A_10=A_01=A_11=0 and both bottom maps zero. Then F=U_1,10∪U_1,01 must carry H_3(F)=Z (sphere glued from two fillings), forbidden inside 3D U_1,11 with H_3=0.

Rank-zero, zero M, surjective A maps, non-injective A maps and arbitrary upper λ_A are included in T13; no positivity or injection requirement is hidden.

## S005-T15 — sharp dimension jump for the bottom joint kernel

For the preceding A_00=Z, other A_p=0, B_p=0 and M=0, an explicit degree-pure *4D* meet-exact cover exists. Let Y_00=S², Y_10 and Y_01 be two separate 3-balls with common boundary S², so Y_10∩Y_01=Y_00 and F=S³. Define Y_11=C(F)=D⁴ retaining F as its boundary. Set every T_p to a based point and f_p:S(T_p)=interval→Y_p to the common basepoint, literally compatible. Apply the same labelled three-cover cone/cylinder construction; all new cones over the interval are at most 2D and the top Y_11 contributes exactly dimension 4. All contractible pairs and pure homology conditions hold. T12/T13 prohibit dimension 3, while this realizes dimension 4: the dimension jump is sharp for this explicit square. No general 4D sufficiency theorem is asserted.

## Adversarial limits

1. The proven IFF is **conditional on B graph-admissible**, a mathematically substantial but restricted family. It is not the full B/A/M 3D classification. The necessary A-latching half alone is universal under the named degree-pure U_1 assumptions.
2. A tempting proof that arbitrary 3D covers force λ_B primitive via L=(U_2,10∪U_2,01)∪(P_11∪R_11) is **invalid without a mixed-grade intersection lemma**: U_2,10∩P_11 can contain cells not in P_10. Individual meet exactness of each named filtration never guarantees U_2,p∩(U_1,q∩U_2,q)=P_p for p<q. Such “anticipatory” membership may destroy the proposed Mayer–Vietoris calculation. No universal λ_B necessity, nonprimitive B 3D counterexample, or 4D lower bound is claimed.
3. For *extra* synchronous-membership restrictions U_2,p∩(P_q∪R_q)=P_p∪R_p for comparable p≤q (and analogous at unions), the relevant MV latching obstruction can be derived, but this additional condition is **not** part of the original programme's one-critical definition and cannot be silently imposed.
4. Correctness is a detailed human-readable CW argument, not a formal verification. A later adversarial audit must inspect every intersection, choice of actual based maps, degree convention and comparison to standard hocolim/cofiber presentations.
5. Novelty remains seriously doubtful: the construction combines well-known mapping cylinders, relative 3-cell attachments, Hurewicz, MV and primitive Z lattices. No journal-worthy original result is certified.
