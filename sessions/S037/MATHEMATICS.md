## C. T79: full relative exactness preserved by critical-degree compression on N²

For the *converse*, pi:P→A has left Kan pi_!, with pi_!⊣pi^*. and the RHS is exact whenever S' is X_A-exact. Therefore E preserves relative exactness and rectangle projectives.

## D. T80: finite-grid deterministic polynomial-bit relative k=1 test (all rectangles)

Let A=A1×A2 with a=|A1|,b=|A2|, N=ab vertices and maximal vector-space dimension d, over F2. There are exactly [a(a+1)/2][b(b+1)/2] rectangle indecomposables; denote this polynomial-size set by X_A. For each rectangle R with least corner s and finite greatest corner t, compute Hom(R,M) via linear constraints on a vector v in M(s). The map is uniquely determined by v and naturality; admissible v have image zero under every M(s→u) with u>=s and u outside R (equivalently the at-most-two immediate boundary exit maps when present). Thus dim Hom(R,M)<=d, and a basis is computable using Gaussian elimination.

Form the evaluation map P0=⊕_{R∈X_A} R⊗Hom(R,M) → M. It is pointwise surjective because principal upsets based at each s are among X_A and Hom(upset(s),M)≅M(s). It is X_A-epic by construction (for each R a basis lift is included) and its pointwise kernel K gives an X_A-exact sequence 0→K→P0→M→0. The standard relative Schanuel pullback lemma in an exact category with enough X-projectives proves
  pd_X(M)<=1 ⇔ K∈add(X_A).
The forward implication compares this X-epimorphism with any length-one X-resolution and shows K⊕P'≅K'⊕P0, so K is projective; the reverse direction is the constructed sequence. Every indecomposable rectangular summand is nonisomorphic to other rectangles (connected rectangular support and scalar endomorphism ring), hence add(X_A) is precisely rectangle decomposability.

This is necessary and sufficient for rectangular decomposition of a pointwise finite-dimensional 2-grid module; intersections/sums and ranks are exact polynomial-time Gaussian elimination. There are O(a²b²) such squares; every intermediate P0/K stalk dimension is at most O(a²b² d), so ALL steps are polynomial in the EXPLICIT GRID dimensions and bit-encoded matrices. Includes **all nonprincipal finite rectangles**, not just principal upsets. This is an algorithm for relative k=1, not merely k=0.
