# S108 ADVERSARIAL — exact pinch and geometric cover controls

1. **Does the pinch change the homotopy type?** No. The composite S^n→S^n/A≈S^n∨S^n→S^n has degree ±1, hence based WE. Crucially it is NOT required to be a homeomorphism. Natural *weak equivalence* is precisely permitted in the HI metric.
2. **Does it accidentally erase the coherent square obstruction?** No. Composition of the homotopy-pushout comparison degree with f preserves 1 versus 0. Whole Q_1' and Q_0' remain non-equivalent despite the exact 1-interleaving between their supported R² versions.
3. **Is the source equator really sent strictly to the basepoint?** Yes: f factors through the topological quotient that identifies *every point* of A, not merely through an arbitrary based nullhomotopy.
4. **Are interleaving maps legitimate whole diagrams?** Yes: zero maps exist in Top_* at every grade and are natural. The two 2-shift composites are *strictly* zero after the f replacement, including boundary grades 0,2,4; no nonpoint stalk can leave and reenter the support.
5. **Are BOTH lanes replaced?** Each E_i→E_i' is a separate whole-real objectwise-WE natural transformation with the same construction. No assumption of a common replacement or one-sided contractible lane is needed.
6. **Can ε<1 be rescued by arbitrary zigzags?** The T274 homotopy-pushout comparison is invariant of entire P diagrams, while an ε<1 interleaving restricts to inverse maps of the four fixed regions in the derived P-diagram category; the lower bound survives arbitrary zigzags. Exact ε=1 does NOT conflict because the four-corner inverse-restriction inequalities become impossible at the cut.
7. **Is Q_0 non-inclusion-only?** No! The finite square *can* be realised by inclusions with B=C as a single subcomplex of S^n. The proper failure is stronger and more precise: A≠B∩C and B∪C≠D. Calling it non-realizable as inclusions is FALSE.
8. **Does the infinite-real bifiltration use inclusions?** No: exit from [0,4)² maps an occupied noncontractible stalk to the point, which is a collapse, not an inclusion. The isolated finite square and full-real diagram should not be conflated.
9. **Any new sharp geometric stability theorem?** No. Exact two-set CW cover descent is homotopy-pushout excision; the folded case diagnoses a wrong cover specification. Relative cofiber S^n∨S^(n+1) is a classical mapping cone computation. Product/good-cover controls do not create novelty.
10. **What remains genuinely unproved?** A nonclock multi-parameter interleaving obstruction for two real *inclusion-only* filtered cover constructions with actual cover geometry, robust under both-lane whole WEs and carrying independently novel HHA application; and the old ordinary unpointed Top^R strict separation. Neither is claimed in S108.
