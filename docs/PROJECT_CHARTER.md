# Project charter — journal-directed mathematical discovery

**Sponsor:** independent research programme. **Publication target:** HHA, International Press. **Scope:** homological and homotopical methods, algebraic topology, and consequential applications (e.g. combinatorial topology, category theory, algebraic geometry or K-theory where genuinely relevant). **Exit:** publication-quality original theorem or certified counterexample with full independent verification; publication is contingent on editors/referees.

Our priority is mathematical significance, not throughput. An imaginative theorem of broad relevance outranks a dozen routine observations. Publication target influences the initial search, never the truth of a mathematical claim.

The selection gate requires a named primary-source mathematical problem, exact objects/quantifiers, what literature already proves, at least one credible genuinely new outcome, plausible counterexample route, explicit HHA relevance, a finite first proof experiment, Lean feasibility/trust boundary and a significance/novelty-risk assessment. No premature selection or arXiv announcements.

The project follows research → original proof/disproof → FIRST novelty gate → Lean formal verification → FINAL novelty gate → Palomar frozen registration → journal manuscript → owner-approved arXiv → owner-approved HHA submission. Every stage is accountable, reversible and may fail. Do not assert a successful journal result before editorial decision.

Success path includes reproducibility artefacts and source provenance. Distinguish proof in prose, machine-checked proof, exhaustive experiment, conditional argument, and referee acceptance.
