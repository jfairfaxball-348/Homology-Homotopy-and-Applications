# HHA flagship candidate selection: not a vague topic list

The next session must convert areas into **precise falsifiable theorem or disproof statements** with original references. A nonstandard but mathematically substantial counterexample can be the flagship. Candidate scoring uses a transparent 0–5 scale for each dimension; total scores do not overrule proof or novelty blockers.

| Dimension | Weight | What counts |
|---|---:|---|
| Journal fit / indispensable homology or homotopy | x3 | Actual topological/homological mechanism; HHA reader benefit |
| Significance / reach | x3 | Resolves meaningful question, sharp strengthening, striking counterexample, reusable new method |
| Novelty evidence | x3 | Clearly delimited recent prior art, no immediate equivalent existing theorem |
| Proof/disproof tractability | x2 | Concrete promising route; sharply testable obstruction |
| Lean feasibility | x1 | Existing mathlib building blocks, no unacknowledged giant foundations burden |
| Expository coherence | x1 | Potential coherent <=30 HHA-page story with intelligible theorem and examples |

## Seed arenas (NOT selected or novel claims)

- **R-A:** finite simplicial complexes, elementary collapses/expansions, discrete Morse matchings; stronger *functorial* or parameter-sensitive homotopy equivalences with explicit limitations.
- **R-B:** nerves and finite-poset order complexes; sharp hypotheses or minimal counterexamples for local-to-global homotopy equivalences / homological descent.
- **R-C:** combinatorial filtrations and homological stability or persistent homology; exact bounds / obstruction theorems that apply beyond toy datasets.
- **R-D:** derived functors, chain complexes, filtered objects and spectral-sequence constructions; genuinely nontrivial correction or collapse conditions.

These are **search hypotheses**, not known open questions. Their final problem statements must come from checked original sources and show why conventional topology results do not already solve them. Mathematical routes may expand beyond these arenas (including K-theory or categorical homotopy) if evidence supports it.

Reject any target that is trivial by standard nerve lemma/Quillen Theorem A/discrete Morse theory without a significant new element. No formalisation is worthwhile until a proof-worthy target survives the initial novelty audit.
