# Binding research and repository rules

1. LIVE remote `main` is the authority; pin SHA and tree, reconcile all competing work and PRs before writes. Read `authoritative/START_HERE.md`, `authoritative/STATE.json`, session protocol and all relevant catalogues.
2. Exactly one numbered research session per fresh explicit owner invocation. A future next-session prompt is **not** permission to execute it now. S001 is administrative bootstrap; S002 has not been executed.
3. **Headline:** discover a mathematically significant, HHA-suitable original theorem or disproof, independently prove/certify it, and progress through first novelty → Lean → final novelty → Palomar → paper → arXiv → HHA submission. No theorem is selected as of S001.
4. HHA fit is an independent gate: a nontrivial homological/homotopical ingredient and genuine application/significance are required, not merely an algebraic-topology label on unrelated combinatorics.
5. Source provenance: unique `SRC-####`, direct URL/DOI, dates, per-document access level (`METADATA_ONLY`, `ABSTRACT_INSPECTED`, `STATEMENT_INSPECTED`, `PROOF_INSPECTED`, `SECONDARY_ONLY`). Search snippets of official pages are NOT full-page inspection; record retrieval limits and avoid mirrored copyrighted texts.
6. Claims: `CLM-####`, with exactly one `SOURCE_STATED`, `PROGRAMME_PROVED`, `COMPUTED_EXACT`, `HEURISTIC`, `FORMALLY_CERTIFIED`, `INHERITED_UNVERIFIED`. Novelty hypotheses and proof sketches remain unproved. Do not turn an unsuccessful search into a novelty guarantee.
7. A proof must state objects, exact quantifiers, definitions, assumptions, dependencies, boundary cases and full reasoning. Independently attack every critical lemma; record counterexamples to failed auxiliary claims.
8. Any computation or exhaustive search must be pre-registered with finite scope, completeness reasoning, independent checker/certificate, code, environment, commands, output hash and limits. Data alone is never a general theorem.
9. No Lean `sorry`, `admit`, unexplained extra axioms or unchecked computational oracle in a formal certificate. Pin Lean/mathlib dependency, provide reproducible builds and report what was **actually** compiled.
10. Formalism must match the paper statement exactly. Additional assumptions, finiteness restrictions or weaker equivalences must be disclosed, not silently hidden in Lean hypotheses.
11. At candidate selection and before final submission check journal fit, primary literature, priority, manuscript length, paper quality and author instructions. A recent-source audit is mandatory at both novelty gates.
12. One isolated numbered-session branch, one atomic authored session commit, ordinary PR, and non-force merge wherever possible. Verify actual parent(s), PR, branch head and post-merge main; zero hosted checks is **not** CI PASS.
13. Maintain one consistent `STATE.json`, decisions, phase/session/failure ledgers, sources/claims and proof/campaign records. Close every executed session with a full report and an ambitious complete *future* prompt.
14. **External action boundary:** do not contact third parties, register with Palomar, submit to arXiv/HHA, announce priority, or claim publication without a separate explicit owner instruction. Avoid attribution to an automated system in repository filenames, claims or commit titles unless required by a relevant disclosure policy.
15. Do not fabricate citations, primary source access, SHA, checking results, journal approval, successful formalisation or novel findings. Note uncertainties plainly.
