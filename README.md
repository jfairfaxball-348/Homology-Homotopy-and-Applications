## Success means more than a formalised lemma

The research should produce a sharp, interesting and defensibly new statement with clear mathematical significance and an explicit homological or homotopical mechanism, not a cosmetic rephrasing of graph theory. A proof or a rigorously certified counterexample both qualify; useful sharp hypotheses, computations, or obstructions may become papers if independently significant. The programme should stop, pivot, or abandon a weak target rather than submit filler.

The journal's [scope](https://intlpress.com/site/pub/editorauthorpages/journal/hha/aims.htm) includes homotopy theory and algebraic topology broadly (e.g. model categories, K-theory), and genuine applications to other fields. [Author instructions](https://intlpress.com/site/pub/editorauthorpages/journal/hha/authinfo.htm) describe EditFlow, the `hha.cls` format for accepted final manuscripts, and a strong practical preference for articles at most ~30 HHA-formatted pages. Both links and their retrieval limits are recorded in `catalogue/SOURCES.md`.

## Research and release gates

0. Governed bootstrap, evidence and journal-specific criteria — **PASS (administrative only)**.
1. **Problem selection and mathematical discovery:** primary-source frontier survey, ranked theorem candidates, select an exact problem, attempt proof/disproof, preserve gaps/negative results.
2. First independent prior-art and novelty audit **of the exact result**, before expensive formalisation.
3. Lean 4 / mathlib kernel-checked formalisation, reproducible build and independent audit of assumptions.
4. Final comprehensive novelty, correctness, attribution and significance audit after proof and Lean freeze.
5. Owner-authorised Palomar registration with frozen version and independently checked record.
6. Standalone journal-level LaTeX manuscript, figures/data, refereeing-readiness, author-approval gate.
7. Owner-authorised arXiv submission, checking arXiv category and copyright/preprint policy.
8. Owner-authorised HHA submission through the then-current journal process (currently EditFlow), with cover letter and submission receipt.

Phases are **not automatic**; each gate has separate signed-off evidence. First and final novelty audits are distinct. Publication and acceptance are aspirations, not assured outcomes.
